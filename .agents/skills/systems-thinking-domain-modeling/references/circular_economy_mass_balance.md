# Circular Economy Mass Balance Specification

## The Four Stages of Kitchen Mass Tracking

To prevent conflating edible surplus with inedible scraps, EcoLoop tracks mass across four explicit stages:

1. **Purchased Mass ($M_P$)**: Total gross raw weight entered into the system.
2. **Preparation Waste ($M_{prep}$)**: Inedible scraps removed before cooking (onion skins, eggshells, bones). Directable to organic composting.
3. **Unserved Batch Surplus ($M_{unserved}$)**: Edible food cooked or prepared in excess of diner demand. Eligible for immediate holding or NGO redistribution under FSSAI compliance.
4. **Plate Waste ($M_{plate}$)**: Food left on diner plates after meal service. Strictly prohibited from human donation; must be directed to bio-digestion or municipal composting.

$$\text{Total Discarded Waste} = M_{prep} + M_{plate}$$
$$\text{Recoverable Food Stock} = M_{unserved} \quad (\text{if within 4-hour window})$$
