## Brand & Style

The design system embodies "Warm Organic Modernism"—a balance between the precision of contemporary fintech and the tactile, grounded serenity of holistic wellness retail. It deliberately departs from clichéd, utilitarian "green" environmental interfaces by delivering an elevated, sensory digital home that feels restorative, conscious, and premium.

### Personality & Values
- **Mindful & Restorative:** Calming surfaces, breathability, and quiet confidence that make sustainable consumption feel effortless rather than punitive.
- **Precision Craft:** Crisp micro-details, consistent spatial rhythm, and fluid touch mechanics reminiscent of best-in-class financial and product tools.
- **Grounded Warmth:** Earthy undertones, natural textures rendered digitally through soft layering, and empathetic, human-scale microcopy.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for conscious modern consumers who value culinary excellence, low-impact living, and impeccable digital experiences. The interface evokes peace of mind, effortless intentionality, and quiet delight across routine grocery cycles and circular inventory loops.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in elemental organic tones:
- **Primary (`#2B4C3F`):** Deep Forest Sage. Imparts architectural authority, grounding headers, key calls-to-action, and active navigation indicators.
- **Secondary (`#C96A4B`):** Warm Sunbaked Terracotta. Used selectively for high-urgency notifications, deposit refunds, return alerts, and primary micro-badges.
- **Tertiary (`#DCA742`):** Sunlit Amber. Reserved for milestone loops, impact metrics, seasonal harvests, and low-waste rewards.
- **Neutral Charcoal (`#1C2421`):** Deep Slate Evergreen Charcoal. Replaces pure black to ensure typographic softness while retaining AAA accessibility across cream backgrounds.

### Surface System
- **Canvas Base:** Soft Oatmeal `#F8F6F0`
- **Surface Container Low:** Milk Cream `#FFFFFF`
- **Surface Container High:** Muted Alabaster Pebble `#EFECE4`
- **Border Subtle:** Low-contrast botanical tint `rgba(43, 76, 63, 0.08)`

## Typography

The design system exclusively employs **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels to create unified, friendly modernity with clean geometric foundations and distinct humanist terminals.

### Hierarchy & Responsive Adaptations
- **Scale Shifts:** On mobile viewports (<640px), primary hero displays step down to `headline-xl-mobile` (30px) and category headers to `headline-lg-mobile` (24px) to avoid awkward phrase wraps while maintaining strong editorial pacing.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Tabular numbers are enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) across pricing, weight counters, return deposits, and carbon savings counts to prevent layout jitter during micro-interactions.

## Layout & Spacing

The PWA follows a mobile-first fluid layout model prioritizing edge-to-edge finger reachability, fixed navigation ergonomics, and clean reading gutters.

### Form Factor Adaptations
- **Mobile (<640px):** Single-column stack with strict 16px (`1rem`) outer margins. Content utilizes full horizontal width with carousel peek-edges (16px partial reveal) to cue horizontal navigation without visual clatter.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid grid, 24px gutters, nested master-detail modules for cart and inventory returns.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** Centered application frame maxing out at 1200px width with an optional 12-column split (8 cols main feed, 4 cols persistent loop & bag manager).

### Ergonomic Safe Zones
- **Bottom Navigation Safe Zone:** All mobile views must reserve a baseline bottom padding of `calc(4.5rem + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))` to prevent UI occlusion by the floating 4-item navigation dock.
- **Thumb Zone Compliance:** Primary interactive elements (filters, add-to-bag, quantity pickers) are clustered in the lower 60% of the screen.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained via gentle, organic layering rather than dark drop shadows. Depth mimics diffused, sunlit physical paper and smooth ceramic kitchenware.

### Surface Stacking
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Warm Oatmeal background (`#F8F6F0`).
- **Level 1 (Card / Container):** Pure Milk Cream (`#FFFFFF`) with a 0.5px border of `rgba(43, 76, 63, 0.07)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Modals / PWA Bottom Bar):** Semi-translucent milk glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90)`) with an 18px backdrop blur and a soft ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -8px rgba(28, 36, 33, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(28, 36, 33, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Action Drawers & Sheet Panels):** Cream white with an ambient upward spread: `box-shadow: 0 -12px 36px rgba(28, 36, 33, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses a pill-shaped and rounded-organic philosophy (`roundedness: 3`) to echo the curvature of circular lifecycle diagrams, tumbled stones, and natural produce.

### Curvature Tokens
- **Micro Elements (Chips, Badges, Micro-buttons):** Full continuous pills (`rounded-full` / 9999px).
- **Interactive Cards & Input Fields:** Generous curves scaled to 24px (`rounded-2xl`).
- **Surface Drawers & Hero Promotional Banners:** 32px (`rounded-3xl`) top or outer corners.
- **Image Containers:** Strict 20px radius with smooth squircle clipping to retain softness next to typography.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Touch Targets
- **Dimensions:** Primary interactive touch targets conform to a minimum size of 48x48px (52px for primary checkout and return triggers).
- **Primary Button:** Deep Forest Sage background (`#2B4C3F`), pure white label (`#FFFFFF`), full pill shape (`rounded-full`), smooth scale feedback on touch down (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Button:** Alabaster Pebble background (`#EFECE4`), Charcoal label (`#1C2421`), full pill shape, hover/tap tint of `rgba(43, 76, 63, 0.05)`.
- **Tertiary / Destructive:** Muted Terracotta line style with rounded pill borders.

### Fixed 4-Item Bottom Navigation
- **Dock Positioning:** Fixed floating pill dock anchored 12px above the viewport base (respecting `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`), inset by 16px on each side.
- **Visual Composition:** Translucent Milk Glass backing with 18px blur, 4 evenly-spaced thumb targets (48x48px tap areas):
  1. *Shop / Harvest*
  2. *Circular Loop (Deposit & Totes Tracker)*
  3. *Impact (Metrics & Zero-Waste Stats)*
  4. *Bag / Profile*
- **Active State:** Forest Sage filled dot accent beneath icon, icon scaled to 1.1x in sage tint with subtle haptic tap cue.

### Product & Circular Loop Cards
- **Structure:** Crisp white elevated card with 24px border radius. Product photography sits on an oatmeal background cutout.
- **Deposit Indicator Tag:** Top-left floating pill badge displaying deposit value (e.g., `+$1.50 Jar Deposit`) using subtle amber or terracotta tones.
- **Quantity Selector:** Horizontal pill with integrated plus/minus buttons, maintaining a 48px height and tabular numbers.

### Chips & Filters
- **Appearance:** Height 36px, full pill shape, padding 8px 16px.
- **Unselected:** Transparent with 1px border `rgba(43, 76, 63, 0.12)`, charcoal text.
- **Selected:** Deep Forest Sage background, white typography, no border.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** 52px height, 16px horizontal padding, 20px corner radius, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(43, 76, 63, 0.12)`. Active focus transition to a 1.5px ring of `#2B4C3F`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 24x24px rounded squares (8px radius) and rounded circles; active state fills with `#2B4C3F` featuring a soft cream checkmark.

### Circular Metric Ring / Tote Return Module
- Dedicated circular progress indicator illustrating returnable container lifecycle (e.g., "3 of 4 Jars Scanned"). Features 6px rounded SVG strokes with sage progress paths and terracotta return alerts.
