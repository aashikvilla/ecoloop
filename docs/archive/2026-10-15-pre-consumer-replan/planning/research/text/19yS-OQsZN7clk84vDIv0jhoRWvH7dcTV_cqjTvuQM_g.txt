GLOBAL SEVERITY / ENVIRONMENTAL CONTEXT
UNEP Food Waste Index (Mar 2024) consumer retail+foodservice+household waste about 1.05 billion tonnes globally in 2022, household 631 million; estimates include inedible portion. FAO 2023 pre-retail loss 13.3% globally (distinct measure). UNEP has cited food loss/waste around 8–10% of GHG emissions; cannot translate these totals into addressable app outcome. IEA Energy and AI (Apr 2025) global data-center electricity 415 TWh 2024 and ~945 TWh 2030 base case (includes AI and non-AI); never claim food waste caused by AI adoption. Potential story is net resource efficiency using AI relative to AI footprint, not offsetting all data-center pollution.


HOUSEHOLD / QUICK-COMMERCE LANE — NEW SPECIFIC CONTRADICTIONS
Delhi 2026 56 households actually sorted/weighed waste for 7 days; 0.749kg avoidable edible waste per studied household-week, not India baseline. New Yokohama City FY2025 household monitoring report published Mar 2026 and linked 28 Apr 2026 reports real AI camera/photo-assisted food management and recognition/data-entry obstacles; Panasonic KitchenPocket launched Oct 2025 with pantry scanning, recipes and shopping. This strongly weakens novelty of 'AI sees inside fridge.' Source municipal https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/kurashi/sumai-kurashi/gomi-recycle/sakugen/shoku-loss/2025syokulosstyosa.html ; Panasonic https://news.panasonic.com/jp/press/jn251001-2 . Korean 2026 nationally representative secondary survey 949 single-person respondents categories 443 pre-cooking, 386 leftovers, 120 expired/spoiled, NOT edible kg fractions: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wasman.2026.115446 . European LCA scenario of packaged vs loose produce: packaging changes net impact only if actual waste reduction outweighs additional material/processing; no India thresholds verified https://doi.org/10.1016/j.spc.2025.10.008 . India online grocery overordering survey 363 respondents https://doi.org/10.1007/s43546-026-01458-1 not a measured quick-commerce food discard effect; rapid top-ups may LOWER excess purchases. Swiggy official Jan 2026 MCP+Apr approved builder program demonstrate existing conversational ordering, not our integration. NoWaste and Samsung Food real incumbents; ordinary reminder, use-first shelf and WhatsApp are cheap substitutes.
VERDICT: FOOD-01 only ADVANCE TO COMPARATIVE TEST of cook-start meal-change with confirmed pantry (no pantry scanner novelty). FOOD-02 causality UNPROVEN and NARROW; no full platform partnerships. Strongest rejection: household inventory burdens and habit loops dominate. Prove whether contextual reconciliation produces actual extra avoided edible mass vs basic reminders/list.


INSTITUTIONAL KITCHEN LANE — MEASURED WASTE VS CHEAP FIXES
2024 Beijing original 656 observed canteen diner meals and 78.8g average edible solid lunch plate waste per diner (different timing and denominator from prep). Study https://doi.org/10.3389/fsufs.2024.1455756 . One Adelaide Australian 355-bed hospital 2026 Waste Management study kitchen waste average 170.4g/meal PRODUCED: 149.3g unserved, 20.7g preparation, 0.4g spoilage; no proof how much is avoidable or AI-fixable https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wasman.2025.115242 . 2024 German two canteens implemented non-AI smaller portions/plates/feedback, total waste declined 151→51g/meal and 89→49g/meal; nonrandom, baseline differences https://doi.org/10.3390/su16020785 . Thai 2026 school evidence and Indonesian 106 student measured plates; food adequacy constraints important. Winnow, Leanpath, Orbisk, SmartQ and hospitals' MedMeals/Thai School Lunch existing controls. Thai planning source https://www.thaischoollunch.in.th/ .
VERDICT: Institutional PREP/UNSERVED exception timing NARROW/ADVANCE DATA ACCESS; generic image waste logging and plate waste coach DEFER. Strongest rejection: straightforward headcounts, earlier meal orders, signs and clinically approved portions dominate GenAI; actual operator records unavailable. Compare 14–28 days menus, meal count, late changes, kitchen/prep/plate edible grams.


SAFE SURPLUS / NGO LANE — SOCIAL NEED NOT AUTOMATIC MATCHING GAP
FSSAI 2019 recovery rules specify eligibility unused/unserved, safe storage/distribution, recordkeeping and accountability; photo cannot certify microbiological safety https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_Surplus_Food_06_08_2019.pdf . IFSA/Robin Hood Army/Feeding India and many local NGO operations already exist. First-party NITI DARPAN self-declared IDs do not constitute verified active capacity/endorsement, so never imply NGO SLAs from badges https://ngodarpan.gov.in/ . Recipient/old-age/shelter acceptance and dietary suitability require institutional approval. 2026 US RescueLens published study: 14,439 volunteer comments from >200,000 rescue trips, demonstrated NLP issue detection; recall/precision values differ between abstract/table (125 labeled examples), so don't assert unique AI incident analysis https://doi.org/10.1002/aaai.70080 . Singapore Jan 2026 ministry says it does not currently track food-waste data for funded seniors meal-delivery services; recipient waste denominators unknown https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/food-waste-minimised-by-matching-delivery-times-and-dietary-needs/ . Japan first food bank certification 2026 indicates traceability pressures; not proof failed pickups.
VERDICT: NARROW to documented eligible but missed handoffs / recall traceability and only with partner records. Strongest rejection: cold chain, safety, vehicle/NGO funding dominate; structured Google Form or existing RescueLens solves information. Do not equate offers with delivered kg, or donated kg with consumed meals/hunger reduction.


APARTMENT WET WASTE / UPSTREAM LANE — SPECIFIC NEW CHALLENGER
India 2026 Waste Management study across 517 operational decentralized compost sites / 2,373 Aerobin units in Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Pune and Bengaluru (FY24–25) reports 2,085.31 tonnes segregated organic throughput with equipment lifecycle assumptions. This establishes real operation and environmental accounting opportunity, NOT a gap in reporting nor carbon saved by AI; original https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wasman.2026.115633 . India 2026 SWM Rules effective Apr 1 require wet/dry/sanitary/special-care categories and special obligations for qualifying bulk generators; site applicability must be checked (official gazette https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/269620.pdf). Saahas Zero Waste and Hasiru Dala already claim audited/traceable waste services, so 'AI waste tracking' is not a market vacuum. Operators' waste collection and compost equipment maintenance may be physical limits. Specific potential gap: reconciliation of collected → accepted → rejected → processed → mature compost output, only if sites actually lose material/accountability with current paper/Sheets. Compare deterministic mass balance and worker training before Gemini. Farm tomato/mango/onion losses and price crashes often caused by cold storage, finance, capacity, disease; do not label all losses digitally avoidable.
VERDICT: NARROW/CHALLENGER only; original physical throughput strong, residual unmet problem/AI fit and operator data permission LOW/UNKNOWN.


COMPETITIVE FEATURE-BY-FEATURE POSITIONING
NoWaste: inventory, receipt/photo/barcode, shared lists, expiry and recipes (vendor docs). Samsung Food: pantry, AI meal plans, collaboration. Panasonic KitchenPocket: AI vegetable recognition and recipes/linked devices. Swiggy/Instamart: official commerce conversational MCP and approved developer program. Household status quo: physical fridge organizer, handwritten/Google Sheet/shared calendars, WhatsApp, basic Gemini.
Institution: Winnow and Leanpath AI weighed food waste insight, Orbisk, SmartQ, hospital menu/order software; manual headcount/portion signage and service staff routine.
Recovery: IFSA/Robin Hood Army/Feeding India, existing food-rescue dispatch systems, Goodr and RescueLens for NLP operational incidents; regulators and phone/WhatsApp matching.
Organic: Saahas/Hasiru Dala/municipal programs, manual weighing and service monitoring. Competitive claim of 'no existing app' is demonstrably false. Differentiation remains HYPOTHESIS: context-linked change at irreversible decision point with independently verified outcome and lower effort.


RUBRIC/REJECTION GAPS TO ADDRESS BEFORE BUILD
Severity of food-waste problem: strong macro scale; candidate-specific frequency/affected population and counterfactual low confidence. Technology: Google Gemini/ADK/BigQuery/Workflows and web hosting documented capability; no project enablement or model tests, no outcome proof. Innovation: overlapping products and non-AI interventions make low current evidence of incremental unmet need; original demo must compare alternatives. UX: not tested; capture burden could be fatal. Regulatory and safety risks: first-party FSSAI, NITI and SWM requirements; no active NGO/pickup/data access. Explicitly withhold project Problem Value /100 and official hackathon outcome score rather than assign precise invented numbers.
Stage verdict A comparative test, B authentic data access audit, C eligible offer process audit, D physical accounting/site audit. Nothing has met winner selection gate; only technical comparison harness and safe demo approved. Tests are NOT RUN, partner access NOT VERIFIED, environmental benefits UNKNOWN.
