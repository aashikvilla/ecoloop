﻿ECOLOOP AI — PRE-BUILD EXECUTIVE DECISION / JUDGING READINESS
15 October 2026, version 0.2. This is a design and research decision, NOT an achieved functioning prototype. Long-term hypothesis: Google-powered context + verified actions + measured resource outcomes could transfer from food to circular community services, water, energy and waste. Near-term: compare three genuine food workflows. Web-first, optional PWA. Google-only AI/hosting. No Android/iOS submission.


EXECUTIVE ANSWER
PROCEED with one COMMON DEMONSTRATION PLATFORM, not three full products: responsive hosted web app with role-switching demo tenant, typed agent tools, canonical evidence, approval steps and gold-case regression harness. Configure household quick-commerce, institutional kitchen and safe surplus donation as separately labeled workflow modules. Apartment organics is a research challenger (workflow visualization, not a fourth MVP). No candidate is yet validated to win. Score technical implementation readiness separately from unresolved problem quality and actual environmental effect.
WHY THIS APPROACH: judge rubric 40 technical+GenAI,25 impact,25 innovation,10 UX. A polished user story with measurable error-reduction/evidence and secure agent tools is more credible than many disconnected AI calls. Food sector urgency is supported; unmet gap after incumbents remains unproven. A single high-quality prototype may be better than broad coverage.


WEB-FIRST AND TIME CONSTRAINT
Web app: React+TypeScript SPA, Vite and Firebase Hosting for reliable HTTPS and mobile-ready link. Optional service worker/manifest only after web works; do not cache household confidential payloads or claims of offline checkout. PWA install depends browser and device; functionality must not depend on install. Swiggy/NGO partner integrations are conditional and simulated with visible labels until authorized. Official challenge rules reference a Google Cloud/Firebase deployed functional prototype, not native stores; check exact latest rules: https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html . Date-dependent submissions must be verified directly with official organizers.


CANDIDATES AND EVIDENCE QUALITY — NO FAKE NUMERIC SCORES
A HOUSEHOLD CHANGES: observed India Delhi 56 homes × 7 days, mean 0.749 kg avoidable edible waste/household-week. Yokohama City FY2025 study (March 2026 report) shows AI refrigerator capture attempted already; respondent problems include camera recognitions, inventory entry and actual follow-through. NoWaste, Samsung Food, Panasonic KitchenPocket, Swiggy commerce MCP and simple WhatsApp/label reminders are competitors. Problem existence HIGH within measured samples; broad prevalence MEDIUM/UNKNOWN; residual gap LOW, untested. Technical story STRONG POTENTIAL; user-per-week impact SMALL/UNKNOWN without field trial. Strongest rejection: incremental effort is not worth it.
B KITCHEN EXCEPTIONS: 2026 Australian 355-bed hospital measured mean 170.4g kitchen waste per produced meal, dominated by unserved food (149.3g) at one hospital; does not equal safely preventable mass. 2024 Beijing 656 diner observations and 78.8g edible plate waste/lunch; different stage. German non-AI canteen measures lowered total waste in uncontrolled studies (151→51g/meal and 89→49g/meal). Winnow/Leanpath/SmartQ, clinical diet systems, headcounts, signs are real. Problem observed medium-high; residual after non-AI and vendors low/unknown. Real-site access serious blocker. Strongest rejection: a QR headcount or staged cooking beats Gemini.
C RECOVERY/NGO: real regulatory, safety, traceability and nutritional stakes; FSSAI IFSA, NGO van networks and nonprofit software already exist. India eligible offers lost to missing info has no measured rate; logistics, vehicle availability and food-safety constraints dominate. 2026 first-party Japanese certification and 2026 US RescueLens research show sophisticated existing workflows. No claims donated kg=consumed or hunger reduced. Strongest rejection: partners and vehicles, not software, are scarce.
D APARTMENT ORGANICS CHALLENGER: India study (2026) covers 517 operational sites/2,373 Aerobin units, 2,085.31 tonnes segregated organics (FY24–25), environmental LCA; observed throughput not evidence software-caused impact or compliance errors. Saahas and Hasiru Dala existing. Strongest rejection: operators already log throughput / cheaper deterministic mass balance suffices.


RUBRIC REVERSE-ENGINEERING — WHAT IS EVIDENCED VS INFERRED
OFFICIAL competition weighted rubric, supplied in project: technical and GenAI implementation 40%, problem alignment and impact 25%, innovation and creativity 25%, UX and design 10%. This is the ONLY defensible official point allocation. Review of earlier hackathon winners/AI for Impact adjacent projects suggests qualitatively that real users, a visible demo, source-grounded impact and convincing story help; no published judges' sub-scores or causal winner-selection model were verified for this project. Treat these as INTERPRETATIONS, not secret scoring rules or proof a feature earns bonus points.
Technical (40): demonstrate deployed Gemini structured multi-input parse → ADK tool use → deterministic permission/quantity rules → human-approved reversible action → audit; eval on 12–20 declared gold scenarios against plain Gemini and non-AI. Incomplete until code deployed and tests run.
Impact (25): sector totals do not equal addressed harm; use edible kg, kitchen g/meal and accepted safe kg with denominators; actual reductions UNKNOWN.
Innovation (25): explicit competitor head-to-head, especially Panasonic AI fridge and Swiggy MCP, non-AI kitchen gains and NGO solutions. Still UNPROVEN.
UX (10): direct URL, responsive mobile, minimal input, explicit privacy/uncertainty and role portals. Screens/unmoderated tests NOT RUN.
No justified full 100-point award score now. Design/potential estimates are NOT judges' scores. Problem Value /100 remains withheld because unmet residual need, intensity/cost and addressable denominator are unknown.


HARD STOP GATES AND DECISION
G0 measurable physical harm in representative target workflow; G1 specific residual failure survives incumbents/manual alternatives; G2 authorized authentic data and actor power; G3 meaningful Gemini benefit over rules/plain Gemini; G4 no food safety, financial authorization, recipient/dietary or data protection bypass; G5 measurable physical baseline/outcomes; G6 live Google deployment and working product. UNKNOWN is not PASS. Current: broad harm yes, precise residual G1 unknown all three; G2 permission access limited; G3 NOT RUN; G4 requirements designed not implemented; G5 methods defined no pilot; G6 not deployed. Approved action now: documentation and benchmark harness specification, not merchant/NGO production launch.
Most defensible first coding slice is an honest synthetic example: dinner plans changed before cooking with verified pantry and cook approval, explicitly mock cart. Maintain B/C in common evaluation so they can beat A if evidence supports them.
