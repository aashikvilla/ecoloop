PARALLEL WRITING PROTOCOL
Each researcher edits ONLY their own lane document. They MAY read every shared document and register, but must not concurrently edit the central one-pager, central Google Sheet, decision log or slide deck. Lane 5 is the sole writer of the central Research Register, decision log, one-pager and slide deck, synthesizing substantiated changes after reading all four lane logs. Preserve dated history; add unique source IDs and never silently overwrite contradictory evidence. A failed source, missing API or inability to access data must be recorded as UNKNOWN or NOT RUN. A scheduled run should produce no new claim if research only repeats old evidence.


PER RUN RECORD TEMPLATE — RESEARCH LANES
Timestamp (Asia/Kolkata), hypothesis ID, user + stage + precise task, source publication date and direct link, geography/sampling denominator, finding vs interpretation, independent corroboration, counterevidence, competitor features + manual alternatives, root cause, why current approach fails, real harm and frequency or UNKNOWN, GenAI advantage over rules/ML/ordinary Gemini or UNKNOWN, data access and constraints, source confidence, strongest fatal weakness, next pass/fail test, verdict ADVANCE / NARROW / DEFER / REJECT. Cite claims inside the lane document.


PROBLEM SCORE (100) — ONLY WHEN SUBSTANTIATED
Urgency/consequences 15; frequency/intensity 10; exposure 10; workaround cost 10; independent evidence 15; unmet need after substitutes 15; why now 5; user/data access 5; outcome measurability 5; track alignment 10. Withhold or partially score unknown components, never fabricate point precision.


HACKATHON RUBRIC — SEPARATE, SOLUTION READINESS NOT PROBLEM SCORE
Technical / GenAI 40%; impact and alignment 25%; innovation 25%; UX 10%. Technical scope should include full available Gemini capability space only where needed: context/reasoning, grounded retrieval, structured outputs, function calling/tools, workflow orchestration/agents, multimodal documents/photos/audio, context reuse/state under consent, BigQuery, Vertex AI / ADK, Cloud Run, event flows. Models do NOT directly perform purchase/donation; authorized tools and user confirmations do. Some tasks favor rules/forecasting rather than GenAI.


CURRENT EVIDENCE GATES (NOT YET PASSED)
HOUSEHOLD: Does plan-change response save measurable edible food beyond NoWaste, Samsung Food and shared lists? NOT TESTED.
INSTITUTIONAL: Is an important preparation/plate-waste failure still unresolved after headcounts, Winnow and Leanpath? NOT TESTED.
RECOVERY: Do eligible safe offers fail because of missing information, rather than transport, cold chain, constraints or NGO availability? NOT TESTED.
UPSTREAM: Is there one commodity-specific digitally actionable loss after existing cold-chain/forecasting/quality tools? NOT TESTED.
AI TECH: Does any Gemini feature improve the decisive task beyond a basic LLM, rules or existing solutions without unsafe assumptions? NOT TESTED.


CORE FACTS (REFERENCE, NOT A CLAIM OF RECOVERABLE IMPACT)
UNEP 2024: 1.05bn tonnes retail/food service/household waste in 2022, 631m households, including inedible material. FAO 2023: about 13.3% global postharvest/pre-retail food loss (different measure). Delhi sample of 56 households / 7 days: 0.749kg avoidable food wasted per household-week. Redseer FY2024: ~6 India quick-commerce orders per monthly transacting user, ~90% metros; no causal waste link. See links in register.


DECISION RESPONSIBILITY
The streams may propose evidence-based advance/narrow/defer/reject to their sub-hypotheses. Lane 5 adjudicates contradictions and proposes a prioritized shortlist, but NO automatic final choice of hackathon concept, partner agreements, external purchases, publication, or destructive changes without the user's explicit approval. If fatal rejection gate is reached, stop spending research effort on rejected hypothesis unless genuinely new evidence reopens it. Keep alternate high-severity opportunities visible.


PRESENTATION EDITORIAL RULE
Deck must carry a coherent narrative: question → sourced answer → human consequence → lifecycle loss → candidate gap → existing alternatives → counterevidence → verification plan → conditional decision. Separate published estimates from original user measurements, scenarios and assumptions. No fabricated benchmarks, survey responses, partner access, avoided emissions, food donations or GenAI effectiveness. Include source URL/year on quantitative slides and speaker notes for clear verbal qualification. Refresh deck only when new evidence materially changes story. Color / images should serve evidence, not mask uncertainty.
