﻿CIRCULAR FOOD, GENUINE ORGANIC WASTE, COMMUNITY INCENTIVES & PARTNER PORTAL
EcoLoop AI design/research v0.1 | 15 Oct 2026 | Concepts and validation tests; not an operational waste service, partner agreement or nutrition safety certification.


1. THE FIVE-PATHWAY DECISION TREE
Food / byproduct condition is verified by responsible person and authoritative rules; do not label food edible from a photo.
Path P0 PREVENT: reduce preventable procurement, over-preparation and spoiled inventory BEFORE becoming waste. KPI kg avoided edible discard (measured vs counterfactual); priority highest when credible.
Path P1 CONSUME: continue intended household/institutional use with verified storage/label advice and user judgment. KPI kg actually eaten, not imagined prevented waste.
Path P2 SAFE SURPLUS: if genuinely eligible, unused/unserved, recipient approves and logistics work, route through FSSAI-compliant donor/recovery/receiving system. KPI eligible accepted offer fraction; weighed kg safely delivered; traceability/recall; where possible actually used.
Path P3 ORGANIC RECOVERY: if NOT safe/eligible to eat and local collection accepts, segregate appropriate wet/organic streams for authorized apartment community compost, biogas/anaerobic digestion or municipal processing. KPI kg collected and processed, contamination/rejected kg, usable compost/biogas actually produced; do not claim every rotten/animal product is safe for home compost.
Path P4 FINAL HANDLING: residuals go through permitted municipal collection/disposal, with hazardous/sanitary/special-care wastes in separate approved streams. Always check municipal operator accepted materials.
Donation is NOT the destination for served food, badly stored food or inedible scraps. Feeding animals and industrial upcycling are locally rule-governed options that require authorized receiving partners; not default home advice.


2. WHY APARTMENT COMPOST IS A LEGITIMATE RESEARCH BRANCH
India MoEFCC Solid Waste Management Rules 2026 became effective 1 April 2026, mandating source segregation into wet, dry, sanitary and special-care streams and adding extended bulk-generator obligations. CPCB portal states qualifying BWG thresholds (such as 100 kg/day waste, among alternate metrics): verify applicability and local implementing rules for an exact apartment complex, no blanket requirement for every flat.
Saahas Bhoomitra community-compost page reports Bengaluru's earlier 100-household/100 kg/day local expectation and cites INR 4–6 lakh initial setups and INR 15–25k monthly consumables as an operator-reported illustration; this is NOT a national installed-cost average. Hasiru Dala Innovations already provides vendor-agnostic on-premise solutions, equipment/operational support and livelihoods for waste workers, alongside Daily Dump and others. An HSR Layout Bengaluru project was reported in Dec 2025 processing around 500 kg/day across 750 households with 20 aerobic composters; newspaper reporting, not an audited experiment or an estimate transferable to all complexes.
Gap hypothesis, NOT proven: households, societies, collection workers and compost operators may lack correct, low-effort segregation, measured handoff and contamination alerts. However, bins/signage, operational maintenance, budget and capacity may dominate; AI image guidance alone is weak differentiation.

3. APARTMENT COMPOST AND ORGANIC RECOVERY PERSONAS
Resident: chooses wet/dry/sanitary/special-care segregation according to local service; does not need new scanning steps for every peel.
Household/cook: can report or correct food scraps classification; must keep edible vs inedible waste separate for impact accounting.
Housekeeping/collection worker: checks segregation and handles pickups, protective equipment and daily mass; avoid outsourcing unsafe work to vulnerable workers.
Resident welfare association (RWA) admin: decides contracted operator, oversight, budgets, contamination policy, service SLAs and incentives; may not personally operate processing equipment.
Compost technician/operator: decides feedstocks, contamination rejection, equipment, moisture/aeration, appropriate pests/odor controls and mature compost acceptance; should be the verified authority for processing rather than Gemini.
Municipal hauler/authorized processor: accepts rejects/residual streams where local rules and contracts permit.
End user of compost: gardeners, farmers or parks use quality-tested compost only where suitable; soil safety and market demand cannot be assumed.


4. ORGANIZATIONAL PORTAL — TWO SEPARATE OPERATOR VIEWS
Portal A: FOOD RECOVERY PARTNER (FSSAI eligible). Inputs: donor/batch identity, quantity, category, safe handling logs, prep/packing time, verified storage conditions, destination limits, allergies and recipient approval. Views: new offers awaiting review; blocked/needs-proof; safe eligibility reviewed by authorized human; offer accepted/rejected reason; pickup scheduling/capacity; time/temperature chain; recipient acceptance; recall notice / custody logs. KPIs: eligible offers, fulfilled offers, kg delivered/accepted, cancellations and why, unsafe attempts halted. A dashboard is NOT a food-safety certification or legal compliance guarantee.
Portal B: WET-WASTE OPERATOR / APARTMENT COMPOST. Inputs: building/collection route, source-separated wet kg, contamination, collection schedule, compost batch, operator acceptance, processing method and final destination. Views: demand, capacity, scheduled pickup, rejected/contaminated loads, equipment status if operator-reported, compost batch progress, feedback to RWA. KPIs: kg wet input received, processed output, rejects, contamination, costs, distance, availability. Waste operator accepts only materials compatible with its process. This is DIFFERENT from NGO surplus and must not share a generic dispatch eligibility formula.
Both: Google Firebase Auth roles, Firestore document permissions, Gemini for messy report normalization and explanations, ADK/Cloud Run permissioned tools, human approval, audit logs and optional Maps travel estimation. Critical missing: real partner permissions, verified disposal addresses/routes, tariff agreements, authorized recipient capacity. Demo uses synthetic partners clearly labeled.


5. INCENTIVE HYPOTHESES — TEST FOR REBOUND, NOT GAMIFY THE PLANET
I1 Household monetary feedback: show estimated or measured money not wasted, with explicit unit prices/denominator; an estimate is not savings until verified.
I2 Practical convenience: remove friction; integrate at decision moment rather than demand daily manual logging.
I3 Family/shared goals: opt-in, no compulsory rankings of family members; never shame house help, children or vulnerable residents.
I4 Apartment collective metric: kg correctly separated / actually processed by operator, include participation and contamination denominator. Avoid rewarding raw kilograms donated or composted because that could incentivize creating MORE waste.
I5 Reward partnerships: merchant discounts for smaller correct quantities, repair/compost credits or council recognition ONLY if contracts and fraud controls exist. Discounts can create extra purchases; control net consumption and packaging effects.
I6 NGOs and operators: guaranteed pickup windows and transparent service economics may matter more than gamification; rewards that create unsafe donations forbidden.
I7 Community leaderboard: compare facilities only when service types and denominators comparable and users opt in; avoid greenwashing and pressure to manipulate weigh logs.
Test incentive vs no-incentive randomized/small crossover after technical feasibility: actual kg edible discarded or correctly processed, baseline drift, 4–8 week persistence, added spend, privacy, perceived burden. No results exist.
Fair incentives: reward verified prevention/adoption/appropriate segregation, not photo uploads, purchases, or unsupported carbon points.


6. SPECIFIC PROTOTYPE D (OPTIONAL): APARTMENT SCRAPS OPERATOR
RESEARCH CHALLENGER not fourth competing core prototype until secondary evidence clears a residual gap. Example: 100-household Bengaluru complex, all numbers imaginary for software demo. A cook reports inedible vegetable peels; deterministic local rule list says wet stream. Operator marks building pickup; collection staff weighs 12 kg source-separated waste and rejects 2 kg contaminated; RWA sees 10 kg accepted into facility. Processing operator records actual treated mass later. No inferred compost output from input weight; no auto-hazardous classification from AI. Compare printed bilingual sorting chart + signage/WhatsApp vs Gemini chat vs authenticated workflow. Reject AI if rules and worker training suffice. Track worker labor and cost.


7. RECOVERY & INCENTIVE SAFETY
India FSSAI surplus food 2019 regs require safe unused/unserved and regulated recovery conditions; care homes, orphanages, schools and hospitals are not automatic recipients. NGO and recipients must be independently verified and consent. Local solid waste rules and provider contracts govern compost/wet waste; model must not direct spoiled food to vulnerable groups or recommend unsafe home composting. Do not make donation/processing claims from a pretty mockup. A physical handoff and independent evidence are required.
