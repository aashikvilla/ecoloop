﻿ECOLOOP AI | USER JOURNEYS AND SERVICE BLUEPRINT v0.1
15 October 2026 | HYPOTHETICAL target experience; observational validity unproven. Cross-reference PRD, TRD and research decision log.


1. SERVICE DESIGN PRINCIPLE
Not all stakeholders are interchangeable. Shop, cook, eat, donate, collect and measure are different jobs. Describe moment→actor→existing workaround→breakdown hypothesis→system behavior→user approval→observable outcome; distinguish actual user data from inference. No auto action or actual impact claims.


2. HOUSEHOLD JOURNEY H1 — PLANS CHANGE AFTER GROCERIES ARRIVE (P0)
A Before shopping: Shopper checks refrigerator or shared message, may not know item quantities; existing WhatsApp/list/NoWaste already helps. User enters or captures receipt/photo; system extracts candidate items; user confirms counts.
B Purchase: Household buys from kirana/supermarket/quick commerce; record receipt as user-approved observation, not merchant live integration. Competing option is simply a shopping list. Risk: duplicate products not necessarily waste.
C Storage: Another member/cook verifies stock and approximate use window; home inventory can be inaccurate or stale; request minimal confirmations not compulsory constant photos.
D Plan change: Two of four expected diners now eat outside. Family member communicates change by message/voice; system updates permissioned plan event; asks whether planned home meal still proceeds.
E Decision: Detect previously planned portions don't fit now; compute portion quantities with deterministic rules; Gemini helps explain 1–2 feasible use-first alternatives, clearly distinguishing uncertainty and verified stock.
F Review: Cook/shopping owner chooses to cook less, store safely under verified instructions, buy fewer items or do nothing. Nothing is ordered without a confirmation screen; no blanket "safe tomorrow" claim.
G Outcome: Household records actual amount consumed, measured edible scraps discarded, cost if known, effort of using product. Reporting distinguishes recommended potential savings from observed physical outcomes.
Failure mode: no one keeps records; AI mistakes package size; unnecessary notifications; consent/household conflict. Kill if calendar+list delivers same measurable outcome.


3. HOUSEHOLD JOURNEY H2 — MULTIPLE PURCHASE CHANNELS (P0 RESEARCH / P1 PARTNER)
Adult A weekly groceries; Adult B sudden Zepto/Blinkit/Instamart top-up; parent/grandparent buys at market; cook handles stock; adolescent eats lunch in school; someone orders restaurant delivery. System may receive optional user-submitted records across channels (no scraping). Ask whether duplicate purchase is confirmed and whether pack sizes genuinely exceed near-term demand. Quick-commerce may REDUCE waste by permitting small purchases; do not assume it creates waste.
Existing substitutes: NoWaste shared inventory, Samsung Food plans, Swiggy group ordering, messages. Research dependency: actual paired grocery records and physically measured edible waste.
Value hypothesis: fewer errors between source-level orders and consumption; false positive penalty must be measured.


4. INDIVIDUAL / FLATMATE JOURNEY H3
Different work shifts, individual vs communal grocery ownership and unpredictable dinner. One user can tag personal/shared items; system never displays another person's purchases without consent. The challenge may be choosing appropriately sized packs, not family coordination. Distinct evaluation group.


5. INSTITUTIONAL KITCHEN JOURNEY I1 — SCHOOL/COLLEGE/OFFICE (RESEARCH BRANCH)
A Demand: Manager has tentative attendance, meal subscriptions and menu.
B Procurement: ingredients ordered days ahead; changes may be visible only in messages/rosters.
C Preparation: chef selects portions; weigh batch overproduction separately from plate waste.
D Service: actual eaters and portions; nutrition restrictions must be respected.
E Excess: distinguish untouched safe food from served/unsafe leftovers.
F Accounting: weigh preparation waste, surplus, plate scraps per meal and cost; compare headcount/smaller portions/non-AI signage vs Winnow/Leanpath and any GenAI intervention.
AI potential: reconcile handwritten procurement, rosters, changed schedules and menu notes; conventional forecasting may do better. Do not presume same mobile household product fits kitchens.


6. SURPLUS DONATION SERVICE JOURNEY R1 — ORGANIZATION DONOR TO VERIFIED RECIPIENT (RESEARCH BRANCH)
A Donor notices unopened, unused potentially edible surplus; records type, quantity, storage conditions, time, allergens and handling records.
B Eligibility: appropriate certified human partner screens FSSAI compliance; model cannot authorize food safety.
C Offer: verified recovery agent checks capacity, pickup window, vehicle, temperature compliance, recipient needs and consent.
D Accept: both donor and receiving institution explicitly accept; do not automatically assign orphanages, nursing homes or care facilities.
E Transfer: pickup and custody/temperature logging by accredited actor, route only if lawful; sign-offs.
F Outcome: record actual safe kg delivered and accepted, not just listed; measure rejections, failure causes, recipient fit, delivery costs and any actual food access effect; avoid equivalating meals offered with meals consumed.
Failure gates: vehicles or cold chain dominate; NGO ecosystem already works; donated food not eligible; prohibited recipient restrictions.


7. FOOD SYSTEM STAKEHOLDER MAP BEYOND P0
Farmers→aggregators→cold chain→wholesale/processors→kiranas/grocery/dark stores→delivery workers→household shopper/cook/eaters→institutional kitchen donors→verified NGOs/community kitchens→waste collection→compost/digestion. EACH stage different waste denominator: kg lost/kg produced, retail kg waste/kg received, household kg avoidable edible/household-week, kitchen kg per meal, safe eligible delivered kg/offers. No universal inventory or one giant agent across the whole chain initially.


8. FUTURE CROSS-RESOURCE JOURNEY (VISION ONLY)
Same resident can act on food, a verified water leak, AC settings or repair decision, but each requires separate consent and evidence. Shared layer: observation→context→policy/constraints→human choice→real action→independent measurement. Google stack may be reused; scientific water/energy/emissions factors cannot be inferred from a language model. Never automatically trade off indoor heat safety against energy savings.


9. SERVICE BLUEPRINT TOUCHPOINTS & OWNERS
Frontstage: capture, resolve ambiguity, suggest, review, approve, observe outcome.
Backstage: Firestore canonical events, Gemini extraction, ADK tool selection, rules validation, Cloud Run proposed action, audit, optional measurement.
People: shopper=source/approver; cook=portion decision; family member=schedule changes; household steward=permissions; NGO coordinator=eligibility authority; institutional manager=meal/diet authority. AI is NEVER the approving authority for clinical/nutrition, purchase, safety, donation or money movement.
Failure recovery: no data→ask / give honest low-confidence suggestion; conflicting data→request reconciliation; unsafe food uncertainty→defer; unavailable provider integration→draft only; no measured outcome→show no saved impact.


10. SCENARIO SET TO BUILD (NOT RUN)
S01 two guests cancel dinner; S02 duplicated milk across buyers but one pack already consumed; S03 receipt OCR confuses 500 g with 500 ml; S04 old confirmed stock stale for two weeks; S05 lunch canceled and leftovers unknown; S06 quick-commerce partial fulfillment substitution; S07 discount bundle that increases total food; S08 privacy dispute among flatmates; S09 restaurant food already served and proposed for donation (must reject); S10 older-home recipient needs special diet (must not auto-assign); S11 mixed peel and edible food weighed separately; S12 ordinary Google Calendar/list already resolves case better.
Each scenario needs annotated ground truth, expected tool actions, user effort, failure severity and baseline alternatives.


11. END-TO-END QUALITY MEASURES
Completion rate, clarification count, real effort/time, extraction F1 accuracy by language, authorization violation (zero tolerated), waste event completeness, false use-soon urgency, actual weekly edible discarded kilograms, institution prep vs plate waste and NGO safe fulfillment. Set numeric targets after baseline. In UI hypothetical mock metrics labelled DEMO only.
