1. EXECUTIVE PRODUCT THESIS
Problem supported: avoidable edible food waste arises when purchasing, storing, preparing and consuming food do not align; 2026 Delhi physically measured an average 0.749 kg avoidable food waste per household/week among 56 studied households over 7 days. This is NONREPRESENTATIVE and cannot be multiplied uncritically into an Indian total.
Proposed wedge (UNVALIDATED): Urban multi-person households and shared homes have unplanned schedule changes, fragmented food purchases and implicit household knowledge. A consent-based contextual decision workflow MAY avert food disposal with less effort than NoWaste, Samsung Food, shared lists, ordinary Gemini and non-AI behavior-change tools.
Vision: expand only after food success into resource intelligence across institutional food service, safe community recovery, water, cooling/energy, repair and AI-resource awareness. Each new domain needs separate evidence.
Positioning: It is an action-and-evidence system, not a generic advice/chatbot or proof of environmental savings.


2. USER SEGMENTS AND JOBS
P0 A household coordinator who buys or plans; P0 household cook who needs today's accurate headcount and stock; P0 other adult household members who alter plans, buy snacks, or eat out. P1 single-person/flatmate households; P1 school/office kitchen manager (separate product track); P1 compliant donor/NGO recovery coordinator (separate, regulated track); P2 community, municipal and utility operators in future modules.
Jobs to be done: Know what truly exists at home; avoid redundant purchases; adapt portions when plans change; identify food that plausibly merits use soon; select a practical next action; verify what was actually consumed or discarded; preserve privacy and control.


3. RESEARCH EVIDENCE AND COUNTEREVIDENCE
UNEP 2024: 1.05B tonnes food wasted at retail, food service and household levels in 2022, 631m household and including inedible content; sector-level not addressable market. FAO 2023 pre-retail loss 13.3%; separate denominator. Redseer FY24 India ~6 quick-commerce orders per monthly transacting user; ~90% metro market; NO CAUSAL QC-WASTE LINK. Japanese measured non-AI interventions and German canteen signage/portion interventions mean GenAI must exceed a credible cheaper baseline. Existing NoWaste, Samsung Food, kitchen incumbents and generic Gemini challenge originality.
Published research ≠ validation of a specific product hypothesis; do not claim outcomes.


4. PRODUCT BOUNDARIES
In scope P0: permissioned household membership; roles; grocery/pantry observations with source and confidence; upload receipt/list/photo and voice/text plan updates; maintain date-sensitive event log; make time/quantity-aware, evidence-backed meal or shopping decision suggestions; flag uncertain inventory; propose shopping cart but require explicit checkout confirmation; explain why recommendation happened and data provenance; record edible discard with manual measured mass; surface baseline comparison and uncertainties; telemetry/evaluation with permissions; India metro sample context.
Out P0: actual merchant checkout, card/payment handling, autonomous orders, live third-party catalog scraping, food freshness/safety certification from pictures, automatic donations, nutrition diagnosis, predicting water/CO2 avoided from arbitrary assumptions, full sustainability super-app, complete farm-to-fork logistics.
P1 conditional: authorized merchant catalog/checkout integration; small canteen capacity/demand workflow with separate evaluation; verified donation partner dispatch with eligibility controls and human approval. P2: other resource modules only after evidence.


5. REQUIREMENTS (P0 = required prototype, not validated user need)
PRD-01 Account and consent: invite adult household members, editable permissions, revocable sharing; nothing collected from a household member without their consent.
PRD-02 Home context: manually confirmed inventory items with quantity, unit, owner/source, last-confirmed time, and verification status; model-inferred items must not silently become confirmed fact.
PRD-03 Capture: receipt/photo/document/text/voice intake via Google Gemini and structured JSON; show candidate extraction and human corrections.
PRD-04 Changes: conversational household plan changes with effective time (e.g. 2 people eating out); resolve conflicts and ask targeted clarifications.
PRD-05 Decision engine: identify possible duplicate purchases, adjusted portions, feasible use-first suggestions, and deterministic safety exclusions; classify assumptions and uncertainties.
PRD-06 Action planner: suggest action with explanation; tool steps never spend money, share private data, or arrange pickup without explicit accountable approval.
PRD-07 Food-impact journal: capture weighed edible avoidable discarded food (kg) separately from peel/bones/inedible material and donated/consumed items; do not infer actual saving from suggested action.
PRD-08 Accessibility and language: clear mobile-first interface; multilingual speech/text where supported and tested; screen reader-friendly, user-editable.
PRD-09 Household trust: visibility of what is remembered, correction/deletion, no hidden household surveillance.
PRD-10 Grounding: timestamped authorized source for product labels, stock facts, and rules. Unverified expiration prediction must be expressed as uncertain.
PRD-11 Evaluation: test with 10–20 declared changing-plan scenarios, compare ordinary Gemini, basic shared list, NoWaste/Samsung capabilities and simple reminders; actual user trials only with informed consent.
PRD-12 Growth hooks only after authorization: partner API adapter, donation eligibility/chain-of-custody, school/canteen workflows behind separate consent/tenant boundaries.


6. THREE ILLUSTRATIVE USER STORIES
US-01 Shopper: “I see we already have tomatoes and milk before confirming a grocery order, provided someone actually verified the stock.”
US-02 Cook: “Two people cancel dinner; I get adjusted meal quantities and use-first alternatives with clear assumptions rather than cooking for four.”
US-03 Household member: “I correct an incorrect inferred item, see which data were saved, and restrict other members' access.”


7. USER EXPERIENCE ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA / SAFETY
A successful demo must show original unstructured intake → structured candidate → user validation → changed meal plan → grounded option and deterministic quantity calculation → explicit approval → follow-up impact recording. Corrections must update state with timestamp/provenance; stale facts trigger confirmation.
Never state that a food is safe because of a photo, smell or model reasoning. Date-label interpretation only from known authoritative rules; use human escalation when uncertainty exists.
No automatic order on real platforms; simulated checkout is conspicuously labeled simulation. NGO handoff excludes served leftovers, medically vulnerable recipients and inappropriate foods unless eligibility is independently verified under FSSAI requirements.
No unsupported medical, child-nutrition or emission claims.


8. SUCCESS MEASURES AND NON-GOALS
Technical: item/quantity extraction accuracy against labeled test set; change-event accuracy; invalid tool-action count; state-conflict detection; latency; fallback rate; cost/request; user effort and step count compared with baselines.
Product: correct decision completion, user effort, decision retention, repeat use; measure rather than presume.
Outcome: actual avoidable edible kg / household-week and cost of weighed discarded food; compare matched or randomized baseline where feasible. Financial/water/CO2e estimates require measured displacement, factors, local disposal and added AI/transport costs. Do not infer hunger reduced from donation listings.
No numeric performance or 100-point Problem Value Score until independent evaluation.


9. COMPETITION, FATAL GATES AND OPEN ISSUES
NoWaste and Samsung Food: pantry, meal plan, photo/receipt features. Generic Gemini: easy recipe/dialogue. Non-AI interventions: demonstrated measured benefits. Reject if no significant incremental completion/effort benefit, inventory decays, or no actual edible waste reduction. Reject unsafe, inaccurate or consent-invasive variants.
Open: chosen Indian metro, actual pilot households, licensed quick-commerce data source, real cost/latency allowance, preferred audience (household vs small kitchen), access to authenticated merchant API. None is guaranteed.
Launch stage: NOT APPROVED. Research stage: solution hypothesis and comparative prototype test only.


10. HACKATHON ALIGNMENT — SEPARATE RUBRIC
Technical GenAI 40%: demonstrable Gemini multimodal structured parsing + ADK tool choreography + consented memory/grounding + evaluations. Problem alignment/impact 25%: strong original harm evidence plus honest, measured impact; innovation 25%: demonstrable residual step after competitors; UX 10%: minimize data burden. One-week feasibility is a light constraint. Score only after evaluation.
