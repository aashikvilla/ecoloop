﻿AI BUILDER CUP 2026 — OFFICIAL RULES & RUBRIC
Verified/checked: 12 October 2026. The live official page controls if older project memo differs.


EVENT
Name: AI Builder Cup 2026 (JAPAC). Organizer: Hack2skill; sponsor: Google Cloud. Program independently operated by Hack2skill, not directly administered by Google Cloud.
Official sources: https://aibuildercup.com/ and https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html


ELIGIBILITY & TEAMS
JAPAC-based; age 21+; working professionals, entrepreneurs/startups; students ineligible. Team size 2–4; up to two finale travelers. The live site lists registration Sep 1–Oct 11 and team formation Sep 1–Oct 14. Registration is currently closed.

Prototype building and submission: 7 Sep–18 Oct 2026.
Prototype deadline: 18 Oct 2026.
Evaluation: 19 Oct–6 Nov 2026.
Finalists announced: 7 Nov 2026.
Final: 4 Dec 2026, Singapore.
IMPORTANT discrepancy: the pre-existing 11 Oct strategy memo described roster finalization as 11 Oct. Current official overview says 14 Oct for team formation. Confirm any account-specific or terms-specific roster limits in the portal. Do not assume existing registrations can be altered freely.


THE SIX THEMES
1. BFSI: Intelligent Risk, Fraud & Financial Experiences
2. Retail & Commerce: Intelligent Customer and Business Experiences
3. Manufacturing: Intelligent Operations & Industrial Efficiency
4. Media, Content & Digital Experiences
5. Future of Work & Enterprise Productivity
6. Sustainability & Social Impact


TECHNOLOGY AND PROTOTYPE
A working prototype, not merely slides or a mockup. Official themes page requires Google AI models such as Gemini/Gemma or eligible agentic platforms and deployment on Google Cloud through Cloud Run/Firebase; the submission page also references GCP. Use Google Cloud as an actual implementation layer.
Source: https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html


SUBMISSION MATERIALS
Proposal/presentation deck exported as PDF; functional deployed prototype; public demonstration video (three minutes); all submission materials in English. The project strategy memo also cites a public GitHub repository and fresh/original code/assets requirement from the published terms; reconfirm these in current terms and submission portal before submission.
Published terms cited in existing framework: https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRm7ChZ6Ij9fG7uFDkxzUMpwgVeBmnQ6cMDnAIEEX84AiLBOOQ9cYbl3S5OzFBbcVb8TF55s-eVpiXb/pub


OFFICIAL JUDGING RUBRIC
40% — Technical Merit & Gen AI Implementation: meaningful role of GenAI, working/technically sound implementation, scalability/sustainability.
25% — Problem Alignment & Impact: fit with chosen problem statement and clear stakeholder value.
25% — Innovation & Creativity: differentiation from existing solutions and innovative GenAI application beyond superficial integration.
10% — User Experience & Solution Design: usability, accessibility, coherent integration.
Source: https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html

Official overview currently shows USD 30,000 total cash prize pool. See https://aibuildercup.com/rewards.html for award-by-award details and potential changes.
