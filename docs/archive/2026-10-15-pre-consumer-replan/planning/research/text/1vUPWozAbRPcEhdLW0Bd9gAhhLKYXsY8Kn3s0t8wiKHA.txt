﻿ECOLOOP AI — MASTER PRODUCT SPECIFICATION v1.0
15 October 2026 · Hackathon research-based working hypothesis · Web/PWA first · Next.js (TypeScript) + Python FastAPI + Google ADK/Gemini · Google Cloud only for AI/runtime. All real commerce, NGO or waste-processor integrations gated by authorization. No physical impact or real-world partnership has been demonstrated. The goal is one credible functional prototype, not complete sustainability service.


0. PRODUCT IN ONE SENTENCE AND THE CORE STORY
EcoLoop is a proposed circular-resource decision workspace that helps households and organizations coordinate what they purchase, prepare, use, safely redistribute, or properly process—and records what actually happened rather than presenting imaginary green points. Food is the first accessible, high-frequency entry point; community sharing and organic waste close the operational loop; energy, water, repair and AI-footprint modules are future, separately validated expansions.
NOT a basic recipe chatbot or camera fridge scanner; those exist. Potential differentiation is a better combination of changing plans, shared roles, cross-channel records, permissioned partner handoffs, and verified impact. The integration advantage is an UNTESTED hypothesis.


1. WHAT IS THE PROBLEM?
The wider food system repeatedly loses value before or after food reaches consumers. At home, the person buying, storing, preparing and eating may be different; changes in work/school plans, leftovers, delivery orders and grocery top-ups create coordination challenges. Institutional kitchens may prepare before final headcounts are known. Safe and unused surplus may face partner-eligibility, timing and capacity limits. Truly inedible or unsuitable organic leftovers require segregation and lawful processing rather than donation. These are distinct, partly physical, partly informational problems: the app must not pretend one AI technique fixes all.
Problem framing to test: information about food and intentions exists, but doesn't reach the right decision maker at the right time with an actionable, accountable alternative; after a decision, evidence of consumption/delivery/processing is fragmented. Affected users: metro households and flatmates; cooks and domestic workers; school/college/office kitchens; restaurant/grocery/dark-store operators; regulated food recovery agencies; consenting community kitchens, shelters and care institutions; apartment RWAs and authorized organic-waste processors. Households and institutional operators differ in safety/data/finance needs.


2. HOW BIG / HOW URGENT? — FACTS, NOT PRODUCT EFFECT
UNEP Food Waste Index 2024: globally an estimated 1.05 billion tonnes of household, retail and foodservice food waste in 2022, including inedible matter; ~631 million tonnes in households. Not EcoLoop-addressable SAM/TAM and not all edible. FAO 2023 estimates 13.3% post-harvest to pre-retail food loss by a different definition; do not add it to the UNEP tonnage. UNEP cites food loss/waste contribution to global GHG (around 8–10%) but this entire sector figure cannot be claimed as impact.
Delhi mixed-method 2026: researchers physically sorted/weighed avoidable waste in 56 households for seven days; mean 0.749kg per household-week; interviewed planning, overcooking and routine-change factors. Small study; NOT India national norm and does not show software intervention effects.
Australia 2026 hospital study: at one Adelaide hospital, 170.4 g total kitchen waste/produced meal, of which 149.3 g unserved; NOT all safely preventable. Beijing 2024 canteen study: 656 observed diners, average 78.8g edible lunch plate waste per diner; not kitchen overproduction. German 2024 two-canteen observational non-AI interventions recorded declines 151→51 and 89→49 g/meal, showing AI is not automatically needed. 2026 India 517 site decentralized compost LCA demonstrates operational scale, not missed accounting or AI impact.
Quick-commerce Redseer FY2024 around six orders per monthly transacting user, concentrated in metros; DOES NOT establish more waste. Swiggy announced MCP ordering Jan 2026 and an approved builders program Apr 2026; actual access of this Google Cloud project remains UNKNOWN.
Target market size (number of reachable households/small kitchens/partners), purchase frequency for pilot participants, eligible donor failure count, intervention effect, subscription willingness, measured avoided tonnes, emissions and water remain UNKNOWN. We may estimate scenario ranges only after explicitly showing assumptions.


3. WHO ARE AFFECTED? JOBS, AUTHORITIES AND PAINS
HOUSEHOLD owner: wants simple food budget/use planning without tracking everyone. Buyer: wants to avoid duplicate/top-up purchases. Cook: needs accurate expected diners and existing ingredients before irreversibly preparing meals. Diner/flatmate: can share own plan without exposing detailed location or purchases. Student/elder caregiver: meal restrictions and attendance may be managed by consenting responsible adults; minors not required to use account.
KITCHEN manager: controls procurement/budget and demand estimation. Chef: controls food preparation and cutoffs. Attendance/menu coordinator: informs changes. Nutrition/medical authority: approves special diets in schools/care/hospitals; AI not authorized to override. Diner: needs acceptable portions and nutrition.
FOOD DONOR: restaurant/school/store offers only qualifying unused/unserved food with custody and handling record. Qualified NGO/food recovery coordinator: decides eligibility, transport and available partner capacity. Receiving community/care institution: explicitly accepts suitable safe food. Driver: custody and timestamp. Regulatory duties apply.
APARTMENT/COMPOST: resident segregates; RWA contracts service and monitors performance; worker collects/weighs safely; authorized processor accepts/rejects and records actual treatment/output; municipality handles residual legal disposal.


4. WHY EXISTING ALTERNATIVES HAVE NOT FULLY SOLVED IT — HYPOTHESES, NOT FACTS ABOUT COMPETITOR MOTIVES
Households: NoWaste and Samsung Food already provide inventories, planning, collaboration and AI assistance; Panasonic KitchenPocket provides fridge/pantry image-assisted functions. WhatsApp, calendars, notes and physical fridge organization are widespread cheap substitutes. Potential RESIDUAL: after last-minute plan changes, ongoing stock truth, multiple buyers and meal attendance may not reconcile reliably without adding another chore. Not proven.
Commerce: Swiggy/Instamart conversational MCP and group ordering, Blinkit/Zepto plus seller inventory and purchase systems already cover getting items. Their principal consumer experience focuses on ordering, but we have not audited every feature or established lack of cross-platform coordination. Quick-commerce top-ups may PREVENT bulk excess; no causal waste link. Potential edge: verify what's already available and what meals changed before user-approved order.
Kitchens: Winnow, Leanpath, Orbisk and headcount/portion methods already exist, sometimes with forecasting. Potential residual is low-cost handling of unstructured last-minute changes in small sites lacking instrumentation; not proven. Strong competing hypothesis: simple QR attendance or signs solve it better.
Recovery: IFSA, Robin Hood Army, Feeding India, Goodr and independent NGO dispatch address donation; US RescueLens research shows NLP issue analysis already in use. Local physical constraints (vehicles, safe cold chain, donor eligibility, recipient needs) may dominate. Potential residual only if actual eligible offers fail due to avoidable coordination/doc errors; not proven.
Organics: Saahas, Hasiru Dala and municipal providers already handle compost and traceability. Potential residual only if collected-vs-processed reconciliation and worker/operator exceptions cause material losses beyond current logs; not proven.
Therefore claim integration/coherence CONCEIVABLE, not 'competitors cannot do this.' Competition review requires task-level comparisons and permissioned observation.


5. USER OUTCOMES, BENEFITS AND ADOPTION INCENTIVES
Household: fewer unnecessary ingredients bought and edible ingredients discarded, less time spent recording/asking, INR of confirmed wasted edible food; reward saved effort and validated prevention, not increased shopping or donation volume. Kitchen: edible prep/unserved/plate kg per meal, fewer last-minute mistakes, cost and nutrition maintained, staff time. NGO: number of verified ELIGIBLE offers safely delivered/accepted (and actually used if measurable), less mismatch and reliable custody; do not claim hunger eradication from listed kg. RWA/operator: actually accepted/processed wet kg, rejects/contamination and compost offtake; no credit for raw wet waste produced. Sustainability across domains: only net independent kg/kWh/m3 change, appropriately account for additional transport, packaging and AI resource use.
Incentive concepts for experiments: 'food money avoided' based on actual receipts and weights; optional family milestones; school/office waste-reduction challenge with safeguards; operator service-quality recognition and potential approved municipal benefits. Test whether incentives drive additional consumption/rebound, gaming or shaming of workers. No tokens/CO2 points created without verification.


6. OPPORTUNITY MAP AND SOLUTION MAPPING
O1 Dynamic household plan mismatch: changed attendees + dated confirmed stock → Gemini multilingual extraction + Firestore event reconciliation → Cloud Run deterministic portion calculator → two explainable choices + optionally user-reviewed cart. Compare to shared list, regular Gemini, NoWaste/Samsung/Panasonic. Physical KPI edible discarded g/household-week.
O2 Small institutional kitchen late updates: roster and menu changes → Gemini structures messy attendance notes → BigQuery/standard forecasting if genuine historical data, Cloud Run cutoffs and constraints → chef approves batch adjustment → weigh edible prep, unserved and plate separately. Compare QR/simple rules/Winnow/Leanpath. KPI g/actual meal.
O3 Safe eligible surplus offer exceptions: donor batch form/photos (NOT safe from photo) → Gemini extracts missing proof and questions → fixed FSSAI/partner rules and qualified human review → permitted NGO accepts, recipient consents, driver records custody → accepted delivered kg. Compare existing WhatsApp, Google Form+rules, IFSA/RescueLens. KPI eligible accepted/total eligible offers, safe accepted kg, rejection reasons.
O4 Organic residual proof: source-separated material → apartment collection worker captures tickets → processor accepts and logs rejection/batch output → deterministic BigQuery mass balance + optional Gemini document clarification. Compare Saahas/Hasiru Dala/manual sheet; not fourth primary prototype until unmet digital gap established. KPI actual processed kg, rejects.
O5 Cross-resource future: energy/cooling, water, repair, sustainable purchasing and responsible AI compute, each separate research gate; not MVP promise.


7. CURRENT USER JOURNEYS VS PROPOSED (SERVICE DESIGN)
A household CURRENT: receive shopping texts → independently shop/quick-commerce/order prepared food → manually guess what's present → diner cancels → cook unaware or plans not updated → eat/store/discard → no consistent weighed outcome. PROPOSED: buyer uploads/approves receipt → cook confirms relevant stock → attendee reports changed plan in own words → Gemini extracts intent → backend adjusts feasible quantities without unsupported safety claims → cook/user approves modified plan or mock cart → separately log actually consumed and discarded. Value only if lower effort and less discarded mass than baseline.
B canteen CURRENT: menu/headcount spreadsheet and chats → chef commits batch → late absences → overproduction or plate returns → separate logs. PROPOSED: late note parsed → compare cooking cutoff and last signed headcount → propose adjusted batch, keep nutrition and diet authority → chef approves → weigh stage-specific waste. Value only if changes arrive in time and beat low-tech headcount.
C NGO CURRENT: donor contacts network → NGO determines eligibility and pickup capacity by phone/messages → receiving organization decides suitability → offer may lapse or be transported → unstructured custody/receipt logs. PROPOSED: donor submits evidence, Gemini normalizes, deterministic missing-field/partner restrictions, human approves, NGO+recipient explicitly accept, authenticated driver logs actual handoff; reject stale unsafe and record why. Needs legal/partner access.
D wet waste CURRENT: resident segregates→collector weighs or estimates→contracted operator processes→RWA receives incomplete or full monthly reports. PROPOSED: digital accepted/rejected/process batch verification from real tickets plus local-language help; worker/operator audit; reject GenAI if signs and Sheets suffice.


8. WHAT WE PROPOSE TO BUILD FOR THIS HACKATHON
One browser-first EcoLoop dashboard with role switch and clearly marked DEMO mode. Three small but complete decision journeys (Household, Kitchen, Recovery) use same secure backbone and gold test harness; include a visual 'organic processing' journey only as labelled future/conditional until studied. One flagship narrated example: multiple shoppers bought ingredients; family meal plan changes; cook approves use-first lower quantity and mock shopping draft; parallel organization can publish a separate safe-unused food batch that vetted NGO approves; remaining inedible scraps routed to a verified-process mock timeline. Do NOT suggest the family's plate scraps go to an orphanage. The workflow links an intelligible circular story while preserving different legal and physical operations.
The developer must initially implement only reliable core actions and comparator tests, then select which pathway to showcase deepest. Mock partners are fictional/demo-labeled until approved rights. No payment, no food safety from photos, no real partner outreach or geographic coordinates of vulnerable recipients.


9. SCOPE / RELEASE SEQUENCE
P0 (show real working tech): Next.js App Router responsive landing/demo/3 workflow routes; Firebase Auth and seeded demo role boundaries; FastAPI on Cloud Run; Gemini structured extraction of text/receipt with editable confirmation; one ADK coordinator using read-facts, calculate-portions, validate-offer-fields, draft-action tools; Firestore canonical state and audit; synthetic input fixture; human review; simulated adapters; separate metric categories; baseline test harness for 12–20 cases per flow; functioning HTTPS link and 3-minute demo. P1 (gated): real provider MCP where authorized, verified NGO capacity, genuine scale input, BigQuery kitchen forecasting when history enough, RAG policy retrieval, PWA install/offline non-sensitive draft, organization dashboard with real accounts. P2 (future): apartment compost provider with real tickets, broader sustainability modules and incentive study.


10. WHY NOW? SHOULD WE SOLVE IT?
YES to the limited technical and workflow comparison NOW, because direct original waste research, available Google GenAI/agent stack, potential commerce interfaces and time-bound hackathon support rapid testing. NO to claiming an end-to-end verified waste-reduction product or generic nationwide NGO marketplace without real data/partners. The evaluation may determine simple behavioral design wins rather than an AI agent; if so, change the solution or choose kitchen/recovery instead. Code-first prototype investment is justified as an EVALUATION and demo, not proof of ROI.


11. HIGH-LEVEL ARCHITECTURE — GOOGLE CLOUD
Frontend: Next.js App Router + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS / accessible components. Host dynamic Next.js in Firebase App Hosting (Cloud Run + CDN managed by Google); prefer App Hosting, not deprecated framework-aware Firebase Hosting experiment. PWA optional after complete web experience. Authentication: Firebase Auth and App Check, HTTP-only session/cookies where applicable; validate Firebase ID tokens/roles in FastAPI. Backend: Python FastAPI on Cloud Run, Google ADK in same service or separate agent internal service, Gemini via Vertex/Agent Platform. Firestore document transactions with actor/source/timestamp/version; Cloud Storage consented media; BigQuery deidentified evaluation and real outcomes; Cloud Logging/Monitoring and Secret Manager. Workflows/Tasks/Maps/RAG/Memory Bank optional only if use case merits. Never let LLM memory be current pantry inventory; never let model invoke merchant/charity effects without backend authorization and human approval. External merchants/NGOs operate outside Google; 'Google-only' applies to our AI/platform stack, not ownership of the food-delivery company. Official Google guide supports ADK and custom FastAPI on Cloud Run and App Hosting supports Next.js.


12. DIFFERENTIATION POSITIONING AND REJECTION CHECKS
What is not distinctive: AI recipe generator, photo fridge recognition, shopping from voice alone, donation listings, compost dashboards, generic green chatbot. What may be differentiated: one action-oriented, role-aware, low-friction food/resource lifecycle; temporal updates and approved tools; separation edible vs safe surplus vs genuine scraps; verified partner rejection explanations; transparent outcome trail; small formal comparative evaluation showing improvement. Consumer-plus-operator roles are not automatically meaningful; connection must be a real workflow.
Kill if no repeated residual decision failure after incumbents, no credible action/data access, model not needed over a short Google Form/Sheet, unsafe/hallucinated item, real impact not measurable, or a fake integration is presented as real.


13. INITIAL IMPACT SCENARIOS AND MARKET BOUNDARIES
Demonstration scenario only: 1,000 households × a Delhi-sample 0.749kg avoidable edible waste/week × 52 weeks × ASSUMED 10% actual sustained reduction = 3,894.8 kg/year, roughly 3.9 tonnes. NOT predicted app outcome, not India representative, assumes constant seasonal behavior and all adoption/retention. A sensitivity model should vary baseline, annual usage, share of customers and independently measured waste reduction. Money effects require basket-weight unit prices; carbon/water require food-specific lifecycle/disposal and counterfactual factors. No conversion of global 1.05bn tonnes into TAM or revenue.
Commercial market: TAM is NOT global food waste tonnes. Pick India metro, identify reachable households/operators, actual serviceable platform access, realistic adoption, willingness to pay, support and acquisition; all currently UNKNOWN. Social impact: safe eligible offers/delivered/consumed with denominator and quality, not number of NGOs listed. A user-centric alternative to commercial TAM is reach and verified harm per participating person/organization.

15. TEST PLAN AND DEFINITION OF DONE
Tests: (1) receipt items/units accurate and edits persisted; (2) meal cancellation correctly changes portion suggestions, no incorrect assumed inventory; (3) an injected receipt cannot demand a purchase; (4) 2 households are isolated; (5) kitchen meal count change respects chef cutoff and diet controls; (6) served plate food not eligible for NGO listing; (7) NGO and recipient must explicitly accept and record safe evidence; (8) wet waste not counted as donated edible; (9) unmeasured CO2e/water never shown as actual; (10) original user journey fewer clicks/minutes than simple baseline or note failure; (11) replay golden cases ordinary Gemini vs structured ADK vs deterministic; (12) web responsive and deployed HTTPS in Google Cloud, no app stores. Live permissions, timing, quotas, error behavior documented. Actual prototype tests have NOT YET RUN.
