# EcoLoop research synthesis, source register and open gaps
Version 0.1 · 15 October 2026 · Source folder: [AI Builder Cup](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1iTwjBm5FrwthFEzuTZ91D33sRSB4ft9v).

## 1. Coverage and review method
Located and fetched readable content from all 42 files in the folder and its two nested research folders: 10 root files, 22 food-research files and 10 pre-build files. These include native Docs/Sheets/Slides and stored DOCX/XLSX/PPTX/PDF files. Source metadata and text snapshots are preserved locally under research/ with a machine-readable inventory. Originals were not edited.

The review surveys each artifact's purpose, major conclusions, dates and conflicts, and reads the product/journey/technical material for synthesis. Long research lanes and large sheet text exports are used through targeted sections and latest verdicts to avoid repeating the same underlying studies. This is not a forensic audit of every sheet formula, every page layout, every citation or the entire web history. Fetch success is not independent factual verification. Claims not rechecked remain historical source claims with their limits.

Files outside the folder, including winner reports surfaced by a broad Drive search, were not silently treated as part of this 42-file corpus. Existing links to Whimsical boards are retained as references; their live board contents were not independently inspected. The new written architecture and journey contracts do not claim those boards are synchronized.

## 2. Synthesis across the corpus
The most consistent conclusion is that broad food/resource harm is real while EcoLoop's residual workflow gap remains unproven. A useful unit of discovery is one actor with authority, one decision, a physical deadline, available evidence and an observable outcome. Existing forms, shared messages, inventory tools, waste systems, donation networks and operators are substantive alternatives.

The food research repeatedly narrows the opportunity: separate purchased from received and consumed stock; separate plate behavior from kitchen production; separate partner identity from current capacity and batch eligibility; separate collection from actual processing. The broad sustainability platform is a new design hypothesis that extends the trust loop, not an empirical finding supplied by the folder.

The technical corpus contains successive choices rather than one consistent final implementation. Earlier Flutter and Vite proposals are explicitly superseded by later Next.js/FastAPI/ADK guidance. API prefixes, directory suggestions, P0 breadth and benchmark case counts still need an explicit decision. Skills and examples are design aids and do not establish tested software, current regulation or a deployed system.

## 3. Conflict and correction register
CON-01 Stack history: preserve older ADRs; the new proposal keeps existing frontend/backend structure and selected Google stack.
CON-02 Status: the repository contains a generic scaffold; no complete domain journey or impact validation is established.
CON-03 Measurement: current catalog impact copy conflicts with the project's honest-measurement invariant; any future implementation must correct it.
CON-04 Policy: universal 45-minute cooking deadlines and blanket food-temperature/time rules are unsupported shortcuts; require applicable, reviewed policy bundles.
CON-05 Tenancy: default-tenant/auth examples cannot be used as production authorization; deny missing authority.
CON-06 Evaluation: 23 seed cases and 12–20 cases per lane refer to different proposed coverage targets; neither is a reported pass.
CON-07 API: /v1 and /api/v1 plus root health must be reconciled through a documented migration.
CON-08 Research confidence: source count, repeated citations and winner precedents cannot substitute for independent user/task evidence.
CON-09 Footprint: no generic carbon factor or sustainability score can turn simulated actions into observed environmental impact.
CON-10 Live plan: the native Sheet is collaborative status; Markdown/JSON are snapshots, with no background sync implemented.

## 6. Research update protocol
One evidence record contains claim, exact source, source type, date checked, population/time/place, denominator, independence, contrary evidence, permitted inference and next test. One hypothesis contains actor/job, failure mechanism, baseline and stop condition. Keep “source found,” “source read,” “workflow observed,” “test run” and “outcome measured” distinct.

New research updates an existing question or explains why a new one is needed. Avoid appending the same macro statistics every session. Preserve an explicit synthesis/version so future readers do not need the full history in every model context. Revisit time-sensitive facts immediately before the relevant deployment, pilot or submission.
