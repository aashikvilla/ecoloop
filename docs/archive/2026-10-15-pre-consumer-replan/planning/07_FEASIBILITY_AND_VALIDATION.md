# EcoLoop feasibility, validation and investment gates
Version 0.1 · 15 October 2026 · Research synthesis and proposed validation plan. No product trial, partner access, benchmark uplift, deployment or commercial demand is established by this document.

## 1. Feasibility verdict
A small Google-hosted demonstrator using confirmed synthetic facts, Gemini extraction, deterministic decisions and simulated partner actions is technically plausible. Its completion date depends on IAM, billing, model availability, supported runtime, working auth and a successful deployed journey. The current repository supplies a generic material-catalog scaffold; it does not establish the product's trust or domain functions.

A complete sustainability ecosystem is a longer operational program. It combines organizations with different authority, logistics, policies, data quality and incentives. Code can coordinate an authorized handoff but cannot create recipient demand, cold chain, transport, composting capacity, a qualified repairer or a reliable meter. The proposed investment sequence therefore proves one decision loop, one actor network and one measurement boundary at a time.

The strongest unresolved questions are residual unmet need after existing tools, lawful and practical access to the right records, user effort after corrections, and AI's incremental contribution. The historical gate scorecard explicitly leaves these unknown. Treat an attractive macro problem as motivation for research, not evidence of product-market fit.

## 2. Evidence strength and transfer limits
The 42-file folder corpus contains broad problem dossiers, idea evaluation frameworks, domain queues, competitor/evidence registers, lane-specific research, PRDs, technical proposals and successive pre-build reviews. Many entries are append-only secondary synthesis. The same study appears in multiple documents and does not become independent evidence through repetition. Fetching every file provides coverage of the corpus, not independent revalidation of every cited statistic.

[UNEP's Food Waste Index 2024](https://www.unep.org/resources/publication/food-waste-index-report-2024) supplies global measurement context. It does not establish the fraction this application can influence. [UNEP/IRP Global Resources Outlook 2024](https://www.resourcepanel.org/reports/global-resources-outlook-2024) supports examining resource use across provisioning systems and equity; it does not validate a universal coordination platform. The [IEA Energy and AI report](https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai) is a reason to include compute burden and energy context in future measurement, not a basis to assign a constant per-message footprint.

The supplied household research cautions against attributing waste to quick-commerce frequency. Kitchen evidence separates plate behavior from production overage and documents effective non-AI interventions. Recovery research warns that identity badges do not establish availability, batch safety or blanket donor immunity. Organics throughput studies establish physical operations, not missing software or an AI gap. Keep original sample, geography, time period, denominator and outcome beside any reused statistic.

## 3. Technical feasibility
Managed authentication, document storage, object storage, container hosting and structured model output can support the proposed first slice. The difficult seams are current membership/revocation, candidate-to-fact confirmation, source version conflicts, immutable approval, retries, and cross-organization sharing.

Run access and compatibility discovery before scheduling implementation commitments. The repository's Next.js version must be checked against the [App Hosting framework support schedule](https://firebase.google.com/docs/app-hosting/frameworks-tooling); a newer package does not automatically have active support. Verify the actual project's billing, enabled APIs, model-region availability and quota. Do not infer them from a product documentation page.

Use a modular backend and small UI first. Begin with direct structured extraction and one bounded coordinator; keep the rules-only baseline available. Add durable jobs when work exceeds a short request, or when external effects require recovery. Add retrieval only after a reviewed corpus and representative questions justify it. Use versioned polling for the initial collaborative status experience. These are reversible recommendations with explicit revisit conditions in the decision register.

## 4. Data and policy feasibility
A household pilot needs received and consumed quantities, relevant attendance changes, authority and consent, and disposal observations. Receipts alone miss substitutions, shared ownership and consumption. A kitchen pilot needs actual stage weights, actual meals served, batch state and its last reversible point. A recovery pilot needs complete eligible-offer records, rejection reasons, handling evidence, recipient acceptance and transport capacity. An organic pilot needs collector and processor records with compatible weight boundaries.

No such permissioned longitudinal dataset is established as obtained. Discovery must secure sample artifacts and a responsible data owner before a feature depends on them. The public hackathon repository must contain synthetic or legitimately publishable material only.

Policy parameters are not default constants. Existing local references assert a universal preparation cutoff and a temperature/time quartet attributed to FSSAI. The [official 2019 regulation](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_Surplus_Food_06_08_2019.pdf) does not support treating that quartet as a universal rule. Require source/section, category, jurisdiction, applicability, effective date and qualified review; missing or conflicting guidance blocks the relevant live operation. Later amendments and local rules require separate current review. This review does not certify legal compliance or food safety.

Privacy design must establish purpose, lawful basis/consent where appropriate, retention, withdrawal, data rights and breach ownership for the actual operation and jurisdiction. Do not present a generic deletion button as proof that every legal obligation is satisfied.

## 5. Operational and network feasibility
A network must have enough local participants at the relevant time and place. A large directory with stale capacity can create more failed trips. Begin with a few known organizations or one site and a bounded service area. Verify who answers, who can accept, who can refuse, who pays and who handles exceptions.

Recovery and organics have stronger physical dependencies than a household software demonstration. Receiving institutions must have current fit and capacity. Collectors need permitted destinations and a safe rejection process. Repair/reuse requires condition assessment, ownership, quoted work, custody and disputes. Energy/water requires legitimate meter access and a person authorized to intervene. None of these are solved by automatic matching alone.

Pilot staffing includes a product owner, implementation lead, evaluation reviewer and a responsible domain/operator contact. These can be part-time roles, but the same person must not silently approve their own safety or disputed commercial claim. A qualified external reviewer is a gate where the domain requires one.

## 6. Time feasibility and scope cuts
The [official homepage](https://aibuildercup.com/) lists 18 October 2026 as prototype submission deadline. As of 15 October, exact portal cutoff time and timezone remain unconfirmed. The [submission page](https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html) requires a deployed functional Google AI prototype and proposal/video materials. The [published terms](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRm7ChZ6Ij9fG7uFDkxzUMpwgVeBmnQ6cMDnAIEEX84AiLBOOQ9cYbl3S5OzFBbcVb8TF55s-eVpiXb/pub) add a public repository, fresh event work and a video shorter than three minutes. Eligibility and roster status require founder confirmation; the terms have potentially conflicting roster-date wording. Recheck the portal rather than assume eligibility.

The proposed submission scope is text input -> editable candidate -> confirmation -> changed meal -> deterministic portion proposal -> authorized simulated action -> honest outcome record, on one hosted path. Optional image/voice intake, PWA, three full lanes, real integrations, forecasting, policy RAG and elaborate dashboards are cut first if they threaten the working core.

Planning estimate only: a focused slice might require roughly 24–48 effective engineer-hours after access and contracts are settled, with an additional buffer for deployment and submission assets. This is not a promise; a single blocked account or unsupported runtime can invalidate it. Reestimate from the first completed work packet. A safe operational recovery service or complete ecosystem is not feasible to establish through a few days of coding.

P00–P04 in the plan describe the demo path; P05 onward are conditional expansion and pilot investments. A checked document delivery does not complete implementation phases. Deployment, payments, partner contact, live transfers and submission retain their relevant human authorization gates.

## 7. Comparative validation design
First build a fixed synthetic corpus with reviewed expected outcomes. Use supplied 23-case material as a starting seed, audit each label and add coverage for the selected journey. The older 12–20 cases per lane target is a coverage recommendation, not evidence that those cases exist or pass. Distinguish dataset size, unique scenarios, repeated model runs and independent observations.

Compare four practical arms when access permits: form/list plus fixed rules; ordinary Gemini given equivalent facts and privileges; an accessible incumbent or documented substitute; EcoLoop with confirmed state and bounded tools. If an incumbent cannot be exercised, report a capability review rather than fabricate a measured arm. Use the same raw evidence, correction opportunity, task objective and permissions. Separate extraction quality from decision correctness and end-to-end task effort.

Predeclare correctness criteria, unsafe-action exclusions and effort measures. Include missing data, stale stock, wrong units, revoked membership, injected receipts, zero diners, late changes, full capacity, expired offers, and duplicate approval. Record model/config version, fixture hash, run ID, actual tool trajectory, failures, latency and actual token usage. Report counts and denominators; use uncertainty intervals when design and sample justify them. Small benchmarks do not establish field waste reduction.

For a permissioned pilot, collect prospective baseline and intervention periods using comparable definitions. Record concurrent changes, seasonality, self-selection, missing measurements and attrition. Randomize or counterbalance where practical and ethical. A useful feasibility sample can expose workflow failures without supporting a population efficacy claim. Register primary measure and minimum worthwhile effect before examining results.

## 8. Economics and token budget
Total operating cost per completed decision includes model input/output and retries, compute, storage/reads/egress, messaging, verification, onboarding, support, transport and disputes. Cost per successful decision uses all attempted-case costs divided by correctly completed cases. A low price per model call does not prove a viable service.

Illustrative token scenario only: 1,000 decisions, two model calls each, 3,000 input tokens and 600 output tokens per call means 6 million input and 1.2 million output tokens before retries. At assumed 10% extra calls, those become 6.6 million and 1.32 million. Apply the current selected model's regional prices and actual usage; no price, credit balance or account limit is assumed here. If a model is unavailable, the scenario is not executable.

Set per-case call/turn/output caps and a per-tenant spending limit. Cache stable public policy summaries by version rather than private stock; batch where correctness permits. Measure whether a smaller configured model meets the same acceptance criteria before changing. Avoid repeated full-corpus prompts.

For development, assign one granular work unit to a short context packet: task, relevant contract, target files, fixtures and acceptance evidence. The default 6,000-token context allowance in the tracker is a proposed reading budget, not a guarantee of total run usage or account capacity. Record actual token use when available and checkpoint before context becomes unreliable. Do not invent account limits or claim that splitting into agents makes inference free.

## 9. Risks, stop criteria and next evidence
RISK-01 Residual pain unproven: reconstruct recent real episodes; stop if a shared list or existing service handles them well.
RISK-02 Authority/data unavailable: obtain consented artifacts and owner; defer unsupported integrations.
RISK-03 Unsafe policy shortcut: qualified policy review and explicit unknown states; block live recovery.
RISK-04 Deployment access blocked: verify project and runtime early; shrink optional features, not trust checks.
RISK-05 Logging burden exceeds benefit: measure corrections and all roles' effort; redesign capture or stop.
RISK-06 AI adds no incremental value: retain deterministic workflow and remove model calls that fail comparison.
RISK-07 Network/logistics dominates: audit consecutive failed eligible cases; do not scale a directory to compensate for missing vehicles.
RISK-08 Impact overclaim: separate observation, verification and attribution; publish limits.
RISK-09 Cost/support exceeds payer value: run explicit pricing and cost scenarios; no fabricated willingness to pay.
RISK-10 Context drift and false completion: stable IDs, evidence gates, single live tracker and session handoffs.

Investment gate sequence: confirm actor/rights and constraints; prove one technical journey; show an incremental task benefit; run a permissioned measured pilot; show operational and commercial repeatability; then fund the next resource domain. Each gate records continue, revise, pause or stop with evidence and owner. The full platform remains a hypothesis until these gates are passed.
