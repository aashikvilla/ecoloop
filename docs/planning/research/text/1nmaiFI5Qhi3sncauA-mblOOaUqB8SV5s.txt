MapMyWalk
Know your route. Stay connected. Get help.
AI Builder Cup 2026 | Sustainability & Social Impact
Partner build plan - working product name
01 / The opportunity
Build an AI walking companion for men and women that combines understandable route information,
recent incident updates, and simple access to trusted contacts. The shortest route may include
isolated stretches, poorly lit streets, or recently reported incidents.
Our promise: better-informed journeys and faster access to support. We communicate uncertainty
and never guarantee that a route is safe. No reports does not mean no risk.
02 / The MVP
Demonstrate one neighbourhood with two or three alternative walking routes.
Feature What we will build
Route comparison Compare walking time, available lighting information, open establishments, and
recent incident reports.
Explainable guidance Show route trade-offs, data freshness, missing information, and reasons for a
changed recommendation.
Incident reporting Accept text or voice reports. Extract incident type, time, and approximate location;
ask the reporter to confirm.
Live updates Alert walkers to relevant nearby reports and offer an alternative route, with report
status visible.
Walk With Me + SOS Timed check-in, consent-based temporary location sharing, and a user-triggered
alert to a configured test contact.
03 / The core user journey
Enter destination / compare routes / start walking session / receive a relevant update / review reroute /
check in or request support.
Pitch: MapMyWalk turns changing local information into understandable walking guidance - and keeps help
within reach.MAPMYWALK / PARTNER BUILD PLAN / 10 OCT 2026 2
How we build it
A narrow, testable workflow with clear ownership.
04 / Meaningful use of AI
Gemini: extract structured incident records from free text or voice transcripts; suggest possible
duplicate reports for review; summarise relevant incidents without personal details; explain route
trade-offs using supplied evidence.
Deterministic backend: apply explicit rules for proximity, freshness, and verification status. A
routing service calculates walking routes. AI does not invent safety facts, verify incidents, or
independently trigger emergency actions.
05 / Technical approach
Layer Proposed implementation
Interface React mobile-friendly web app with map, route comparison, report form, walking
session, and SOS controls.
Backend Spring Boot APIs for incidents, route assessment, walking sessions, check-ins, and alerts.
AI + data Gemini for extraction and explanations; Firestore for incident records and session state.
Maps + updates Choose a routing provider after checking walking-route coverage, credentials, and costs.
Foreground location updates; server-sent events or polling.
Deployment Cloud Run. Clearly distinguish real map data from synthetic incident and lighting data.
06 / Proposed division of work
Owner Responsibilities
Sudikhya Backend APIs, Gemini integration, route-assessment rules, incident lifecycle, and
deployment.
Partner Map interface, route comparison, reporting form, walking-session UI, and check-in/SOS
interactions.
Both Synthetic dataset, API contracts, integration testing, user feedback, pitch deck, and
demo recording.
07 / Scope boundaries
The MVP demonstrates foreground operation and alerts to a configured test contact. Reliable
background tracking, automatic emergency escalation, and direct emergency-service integration are
outside the initial scope.
Stretch goals: multilingual reporting, smartwatch/band SOS trigger, and background tracking. Any

09 / Three-minute demo
0:00-0:30 Explain the problem; enter a destination and compare two routes.
0:30-1:00 Show reasons, uncertainty, and data freshness; start a walk.
1:00-1:45 Introduce a labelled simulated incident; explain the update and offer a reroute.
1:45-2:20 Trigger a test-contact alert and show actual delivery status.
2:20-3:00 Show report expiry, evaluation results, and next steps.
10 / Trust and validation
Reports carry status and timestamps, expire under defined rules, and support moderation. An AI
classification is not verification. Collect observed behaviour rather than identity-based suspicion. Public
reports exclude identifying personal information and exact victim locations.
Location sharing requires consent and ends with the session. Treat notification failure as visible failure;
do not imply help is on the way without confirmation.
Measure: incident extraction accuracy, alert latency, duplicate handling, reroute behaviour, and
notification delivery. Test denied location permission, network loss, stale reports, contradictory reports,
and unavailable route data. Report measured results; do not claim crime reduction without field
evidence.
Submission package: deployed prototype, proposal deck exported to PDF, and a three-minute demo. Confirm
exact requirements and cutoff in the competition dashboard before submitting.
