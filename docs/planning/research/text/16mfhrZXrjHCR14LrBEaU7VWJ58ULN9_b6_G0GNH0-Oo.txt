﻿AI BUILDER CUP 2026 — MASTER RESEARCH OPERATING INSTRUCTIONS
Updated 12 October 2026. Scope: all six tracks; JAPAC first, global second.


MISSION
Identify evidence-backed consequential problems before proposing solutions or choosing a preferred theme.


RESEARCH PRIORITIES
1 Severity, urgency and consequences of inaction.
2 Independent corroboration and evidence quality.
3 Root causes and reasons existing solutions have not closed the gap.
4 Direct competitors, adjacent tools, open-source projects, internal tools, manual workarounds and the status quo.
5 Measurable customer, business, societal or environmental impact.
6 Originality and credible meaningful AI differentiation.
7 Bottom-up TAM/SAM/SOM or population reach and measurable public impact.
8 Official hackathon judging criteria.
9 Data access and technical feasibility, with a one-week prototype check only as a light constraint.


EIGHT RESEARCH STAGES
1. Map all tracks into domains, stakeholder groups, workflows and source avenues.
2. Collect secondary pain signals from first-party reports, research papers, regulators, public datasets, credible industry analysis, app/software reviews, forums, GitHub issue trackers and user discussions. Cluster without proposing solutions.
3. Test severity, frequency, affected population, costs of failure and root cause.
4. Investigate all substitutes and the structural reasons users remain underserved; seek counterevidence.
5. Estimate TAM/SAM/SOM or reach/impact with documented formulas, assumptions and ranges.
6. Score Problem Value independently of the official rubric. Record feasibility separately.
7. Red-team each leader and record rejection reasons, confidence and evidence needed to change decisions.
8. Shortlist only after comparable all-track review. Recommend primary user research only for advanced problems.


PROBLEM VALUE SCORE / 100
Urgency and consequences 15
Frequency and intensity 10
Affected population/exposure 10
Workaround cost/willingness to pay 10
Evidence quality and independent corroboration 15
Unmet need after alternatives 15
Why now 5
Access to users/data/artifacts 5
Ability to measure outcomes 5
Track alignment 10
Score each dimension 0–5, weighted points = rating × weight / 5. Blank means unassessed, not zero.


OFFICIAL RUBRIC, SEPARATE
Technical merit and GenAI implementation 40%; problem alignment and impact 25%; innovation and creativity 25%; UX and solution design 10%.


FATAL REJECTION GATES
Rule violation or wrong official theme; unsupported core claim; no defensible competitive gap; no meaningful GenAI role; no credible way to demonstrate the essential mechanism. A high score never overrides a fatal weakness.


EVIDENCE POLICY
Record link, publisher, publication/check dates, geography, claim and claim type for every material fact. Separate official facts, first-person reports, independent studies, vendor marketing, interpretation, assumption and unknown. Deduplicate syndicated claims and actively seek counterevidence. Never turn a few reviews into prevalence estimates or invent interviews/data.


STAGE OUTPUT CONTRACT
Executive findings; clickable evidence register; specific users and problem hypotheses; current workflow; severity/frequency/consequences; root causes and alternatives; size/impact assumptions; confidence and objections; advance/narrow/defer/reject; next research action.


WORKFLOW COMMANDS
MAP TRACKS; DISCOVER [TRACK]; VALIDATE [PROBLEM ID]; COMPETITORS [PROBLEM ID]; SIZE [PROBLEM ID]; SCORE [PROBLEM ID]; RED TEAM [PROBLEM ID]; COMPARE [IDS]; PRIMARY RESEARCH [ID]; SCOPE [ID].


STABLE IDS
BFSI, RET, MFG, MED, WORK, SUS for tracks; RET-01 for problem hypotheses; E-0001 for sources; C-0001 for competitors. Research queue is not validated evidence. Leave scores empty until investigated.

RESEARCH EXECUTION PROTOCOL — UPDATE 12 OCTOBER 2026


A research stream is a logical track (BFSI, Retail, Manufacturing, Media, Future of Work, Sustainability, or Google Cloud capabilities), not an autonomous agent. Work and Google Drive changes happen during active ChatGPT execution; do not claim asynchronous background progress without a scheduled automation.


Completion levels:
L0 Domain mapped; L1 evidence of broad burden; L2 defined affected user and observed workflow signal; L3 independent corroboration + root-cause investigation; L4 direct competitors, substitutes, counterevidence, and a remaining unmet gap tested; L5 measurement/sizing and primary validation. No problem is 'validated' at L0-L2.


Every dossier MUST record (a) exact user and job, (b) observed denominator/geography/date, (c) source type and independence (including duplicates), (d) frequency/severity/consequences, (e) plausible versus observed root cause, (f) incumbent and manual alternatives, (g) strongest rejection reason, (h) sizing formula with missing input values, (i) status and next falsifiable check.


Do not use a source count as a quality metric. Dashboard separate metrics: queued questions, raw source records, independently corroborated cases, named competitor coverage, completed preliminary dossiers, demonstrated residual unmet needs, problem-value scores only when justified, and actual last edit timestamp. Snapshot counts must explicitly state last verified date. Never treat zero validated as work not attempted when a preliminary research pass has been completed.


Competition capability stream stays separate: document actual Gemini/Google Cloud functionality, limitations, region/price/preview/access, and non-Google alternatives; require measured technical evidence before claiming unique advantage or technical wow factor. The official 40% technical merit category is scored on a demonstrable future solution, not used to bias problem evidence.
