SERVICE ECOSYSTEM — ACTORS AND AUTHORITY
HOUSEHOLD: account owner controls invite/share consent; adult buyer confirms prices and inventory; cook controls actual cooking plan; diners report attendance only; domestic worker gets minimum stock/prep permission, no household spending surveillance. Young family members not required to register or disclose personal schedules. Kitchen: facility manager controls cost/policies, meal planner plans attendance, chef confirms prep, dietitian/clinician controls therapeutic diets, diner controls preference and privacy. Recovery: donor offers only safe unused/unserved potential batch with verified holding logs, qualified NGO/recovery agency decides eligibility and capacity, driver confirms pickup custody, receiving organization consents to food/diet/temperature and records receipt, compliance auditor can read necessary audit data. Organics: resident segregates according to local service, RWA approves budget, authorized collector logs wet waste and contamination, operator accepts/rejects/processes, municipal contractor manages rejects and final disposal. No AI authority over food microbiology, pay, recipient/clinical care or disposal legal licensing.


JOURNEY A — METRO HOUSEHOLD / QUICK COMMERCE (END TO END)
T0 Invite: one adult creates a synthetic or consented household, picks language and invites cook/family members. Default sharing limited. Existing substitute WhatsApp/Calendar.
T1 Shopping intent: buy weekly vegetables from kirana or limited quick-commerce top-up, optionally uploads a receipt / types list. Gemini returns named candidate items and quantity evidence; user corrects unit and confirms. Partial fulfillment/substitution cannot be silently treated as delivered stock.
T2 Stock: data is confirmed with quantity, observed_at, source and stale warning. Another buyer's purchase is not shared without consent; consumed stock must be updated or flagged unknown. Current incumbents NoWaste, Samsung Food, Panasonic KitchenPocket already cover much inventory functionality.
T3 Changed situation: two diners report working late/eating outside AFTER ingredients were bought, before cooking cutoff. Gemini parses optional voice/text; an event log records who approved and which meal changed. Real data not available in test; synthetic demo disclosed.
T4 Plan reconciliation: Cloud Run compares confirmed portions vs dining count and use-first rules; Gemini explains two safe and feasible options without overclaiming freshness. When stock uncertain, asks 1 targeted question.
T5 Buying action: optional cart comparison and suggestions. Swiggy official MCP and Builders Club indicate potential approved integration but our account rights unknown. DEMO only shows a mock cart, clear full price, substitution restrictions and user approval; no real checkout.
T6 Post-event: user records physically weighed edible discarded g and purchase/refund data, or explicitly 'Not measured'; system does not display fictional water/CO2e savings. Evaluation compares rules/shared list, ordinary Gemini and current incumbents with same inputs.
Pain points: data entry; pantry truth; multiple buyers; wrong item sizes; cook authority; privacy; frequent notifications. Trigger if initial test fails: kill agent layer and propose low-effort habit design, not more features.


JOURNEY B — INSTITUTIONAL CANTEEN OR HOSPITAL (END TO END)
T0 Organization manager opens consented isolated workspace; no student/patient identifiers in demo; chef has kitchen control, attendance controller edit-only schedule.
T1 Planning: menu and contracted meals, historical counts, ingredients in typed Google Sheets or uploaded source, normalized with Gemini but verified manually; nutritional standards kept as hard policies. Competitor Winnow/Leanpath/SmartQ plus simple QR headcounts and signage.
T2 Exception: absentee group, exam cancellation, change of ward diet or stock substitution enters as text/voice note before cooking cutoff. Gemini extracts change and provenance; clinical nutrition changes require qualified staff approval.
T3 Count: deterministic rules and optionally BigQuery time-series estimate meals, compare exact inputs to manual/QR baseline; option to stage cooking rather than blindly cut portions. Chef sees original, proposed, expected/actual servings, warning if change arrives too late.
T4 Review: manager/chef approves versioned safe plan, cannot auto-change patient therapeutic requirements. Record whether adjusted batch was ACTUALLY cooked; simulated demo cannot pretend a hospital changed production.
T5 Waste: operator separately weighs edible prep waste, unused safe production, exposed buffet, diner plate, inedible and packaging; divide by actual meals served. Record calories/nutrition adequacy and user satisfaction. Physical result requires a multiweek pilot.
Failure/kill: most plate waste caused by taste/appetite, not attendance; basic QR list more accurate; changes arrive too late; no site data or authority. Do not equate all gross food waste with avoidable edible waste.


JOURNEY C — SAFE FOOD SURPLUS / NGO / RECIPIENT (END TO END)
T0 Donor account and qualified registered recovery/receiving agency linked by human-admin permission and suitability. In mock mode everything is fictitious and marked.
T1 Possible unused safe surplus discovered at restaurant, school, retailer or event. Donor submits offered category, batch, weight, origin, packing, handling time, validated temp logs, allergen and deadline; Gemini may parse unstructured description but never pronounce safe.
T2 Deterministic mandatory-field checker validates data completeness and locally approved eligibility policy; if served food or handling uncertain → REJECT or HUMAN REVIEW, NOT safe donation. FSSAI/partner staff make actual decision. Distinguish unopened produce policies and cooked-food policies by partner.
T3 Verified NGO capacity and vehicle/time window checked, not inferred from map result. An agency can decline with reason and a later alternative operator is proposed only if suitable and independently verified. No orphanage, hospital or aged home auto-assigned.
T4 Human donor + NGO + recipient approvals, custody status and ability to cancel; Workflows optional for multi-hour callbacks; Cloud Run handles idempotent storage; agent cannot repeat pickup payments.
T5 Partner confirms pickup, weight, packaging, custody and temperature, recipient signs acceptance and records consumption only if actually measured; a lost/returned batch recorded with reason, not 'saved'. Audit/recall notice available with scoped roles and privacy. Compare phone+WhatsApp, forms+rules, existing IFSA networks vs Gemini intake.
Kill if vans, cold storage, eligibility, charity finance dominate; if advanced existing vendor (e.g. RescueLens/IFSA) already solves failure; if no eligible-offer denominator or recipient consent.


JOURNEY D — GENUINE ORGANIC WASTE / APARTMENT COMMUNITY (CHALLENGER)
T0 RWA identifies regulated obligations and service contract with authorized waste operator; qualified bulk-generator rules/site applicability confirmed locally; not every apartment must install on-site compost equipment.
T1 Household separates wet material that is genuinely inedible or unsuitable for recovery from safe edible surplus; user only sees approved local accepted-material instructions, no AI food safety inference.
T2 Collector weighs source-segregated stream and records contamination; operator signs accepted vs rejected kg. Gemini may normalize multilingual notes, but simpler icons/signage may suffice. Worker PPE and consent matter.
T3 Composter/biogas service logs material actually processed, residual reject load, energy/water inputs, matured compost and validated eventual end use. RWA sees raw collected vs accepted vs processed vs output, not fictitious diversion. Incentives reward correctly verified processing/reduced contamination and waste prevention, not raw kg thrown away.
T4 Reconcile official waste rules, lawful disposal and local operator contract; audit generated with mass balance deterministic BigQuery/SQL, not invented AI emissions. India 2026 517-site observational study shows this already has practiced LCA and service providers, so residual gap unknown.
Kill if Saahas/Hasiru Dala and simple logs provide adequate proof or if contamination/physical capacity is key issue.


MULTI-TENANT SERVICE BLUEPRINT
Frontstage: home dashboard (consented); organizational role switch; capture and edit extracted fields; uncertainty display; proposal and approvals; confirmed outcome evidence and small useful visual impact. Backstage: browser request with Firebase session → Cloud Run validates role/scope → Firestore reads canonical facts → Gemini+ADK optional interpretation and tool proposal → deterministic quantity/legal policy → human approves → mock partner or approved external integration → Firestore event audit → BigQuery aggregate → evaluation. Missing evidence defaults to 'unknown' not made-up. Each action includes data source, actor, version and timestamp. Errors: source lost→manual entry; conflicting versions→choose latest verified with prompt; unauthorized→deny; stale food storage→human refer; partner unavailable→pending/cancel; no scale→unmeasured outcome.


INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE / KEY PAGES
/ Welcome: explicit sustainability social/environment thesis; current demo privacy. /demo choose A B C seeded cases. /household inventory and 'plans changed' decision. /kitchen headcount and preparation exception. /recovery donor offers, NGO inbox, recipient approval and custody timeline. /organics research only, not launch. /impact separated physical categories, uncertain amounts. /eval compare baselines and agent traces. /settings roles, personal context and deletion. All primary screens mobile responsive and keyboard accessible. Avoid tabs promising functioning payment or automatic donation.


MEASUREMENT AND SOURCE LINKS
A primary: measured g avoidable edible discarded / household-week, and g / kg edible purchased. B: separately prepared, unserved and plate g edible waste / actually served meal, plus nutritional suitability. C: eligible human-approved offer count, successful handoffs and kg delivered/accepted, whether consumed known, and safety/transport failures. D: collected, rejected, actually processed, output kg and lawful destination. Social impacts: household INR net wasted, staff/volunteer hours, institutional meal adequacy, optional eligible people actually served. Environmental avoided CO2e/m3 water only via defensible counterfactual, food-specific factors and added transport/compute; otherwise do not report.
