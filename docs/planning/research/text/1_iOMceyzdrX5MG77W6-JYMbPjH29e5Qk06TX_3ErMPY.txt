OPENING QUESTION: What if improving everyday food decisions could avoid wasting resources already invested in growing, transporting and cooking food? This is a research narrative, not a product launch. Frame as environmental impact plus household economics/food access. We have NOT demonstrated an AI product reduces waste.

QUESTION: How large is consumer-level food waste? This includes inedible portions. Do not equate this with preventable waste or potential market. Source: https://wedocs.unep.org/handle/20.500.11822/45230

QUESTION: Why is wasted food climate relevant? UNEP estimates food loss and waste contribute roughly 8–10% of global greenhouse gas emissions. QUESTION: Is there locally measured evidence, not just global estimates? A Delhi study weighed waste for seven days in 56 households and found a mean 0.749kg/week of avoidable waste, citing poor planning, overcooking, shared coordination, and changed social/work plans. Small nonrepresentative sample; do not extrapolate as India average. Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0950329325003064

QUESTION: Does India's quick-commerce boom make food waste better or worse? Redseer FY2024 reported ~6 q-commerce orders per monthly transacting user and ~90% metro share. This establishes frequency/market structure, NOT caused waste. Rapid top-ups could reduce bulk spoilage; promotions might increase unnecessary buying. We need actual purchases + weighed disposal evidence. Source: https://redseer.com/articles/indias-q-commerce-ascent-a-new-era-in-shopping/

Competing apps NoWaste and Samsung Food already support lists/meal planning/inventory, so gap is still unverified. Measure real user effort, food discarded and alternative performance. QUESTION: Could one school/canteen have a larger observable impact than a single home? Institutional meal demand varies by attendance, preferences, portion sizes and diets. Measure food before and after serving separately. Compete against simple headcounts, Winnow and Leanpath; do not claim AI detection/forecasting are new. Main barrier could be changing meals/attendance, user operations, or lack of budget. Sources: https://www.winnowsolutions.com/ and https://www.leanpath.com/

QUESTION: Can surplus feed people rather than reach a landfill? Only eligible safe unused surplus is considered for donation, and acceptance, refrigeration, allergens, time/temperature, and institution needs control safety. India already has FSSAI Indian Food Sharing Alliance and NGO operations. We cannot equate posted kg with consumed meals or hunger prevented. FSSAI 2019: https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_Surplus_Food_06_08_2019.pdf

10 / AI JUSTIFICATION

Official Gemini API / Google Cloud docs; advantage not proven.
11
MORE

Context, tool orchestration, authorized actions, structured memory, grounding and multimodal input.
Human approval for purchases or donation
Rules/ML for calculation, safety and forecasting

QUESTION: Does the technical merit require only image understanding? No. Google's official Gemini capabilities include structured output, grounded external data, function calling, voice/audio/images/video/documents and tool-based workflows. Persistent context must be explicitly implemented under consent. Algorithms/rules can be more appropriate for quantities, food safety and forecasting. Model has no inherent payment/donation privileges; app tools must execute authorized actions. Official docs: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/function-calling and https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/structured-output

QUESTION: What evidence would change the decision? Pass if an actual, consequential, measurable food-waste failure persists after existing products, and GenAI or agentic workflows improve it safely and materially. Need measured kg and credible baseline, user effort, comparator test, privacy/safety checks, and honest accounting. Official judging weights 40% GenAI technical, 25% impact, 25% innovation, 10% UX; Problem Value Score remains independent. No benchmark, donations or AI savings tested yet.
