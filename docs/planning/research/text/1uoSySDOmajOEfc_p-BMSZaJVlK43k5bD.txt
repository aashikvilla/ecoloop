Delhi measured household waste study, Feb 2026 issue · small nonrepresentative sample
A Delhi mixed-method study collected and weighed food waste in 56 households for seven days and reported 0.749 kg of avoidable waste per household per week. Interviews identified poor planning, overcooking, household coordination and unexpected work/social changes. This is not a nationally representative household baseline and should not be extrapolated across India. Original https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0950329325003064 . Competitors already support shared inventory, meal planning and receipt/photo capture: https://www.nowasteapp.com/home/ and https://support.samsungfood.com/ . Quick top-ups might reduce bulk overbuying while promotions might increase unneeded purchases. Need matched orders and weighed waste. Agent orchestration ≠ permission. Gemini suggests actions; authenticated systems execute them.
Google Gemini API function calling, structured output and ADK docs · capability, not proven impact
Technology is a means to an observed decision. Gemini can parse unstructured inputs, produce structured outputs, use grounded retrieval and propose function calls. The application authenticates and executes tools; agents do not possess merchant purchase or NGO pickup authority by default. ADK can orchestrate sequential workflows and maintain application-managed session state, but this alone is not novel. Deterministic code should compute quantities, apply food-safety checks and enforce human approval; conventional ML/operations research may better handle forecasting and routing. Compare against ordinary Gemini, forms, rules, simple forecasts and current vendor products on identical test cases. Sources https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/function-calling ; https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/structured-output ; https://adk.dev/agents/workflow-agents/sequential-agents/ . Research protocol draft, 14 Oct 2026; no measured AI effectiveness claimed
Household: compare existing apps/shared lists and ordinary Gemini on actual changes, with a weighed discard baseline. Campus: separately weigh prep, serving and plate waste, compare headcount and simple forecast. Recovery: count all consecutive offers including rejected/expired and identify eligibility and true information bottlenecks; never claim listed kg = consumed kg. Tomato: classify physical handling, price/economics and time-sensitive information loss; compare e-NAM, phone, rules and Gemini. These are proposed tests, NOT performed.
